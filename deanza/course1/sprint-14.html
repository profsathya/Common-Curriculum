Sprint 1: Find the problem worth solvingFraming▸
Module Learning Goals
  • Build a broad list from your own week, then describe three to five candidate gaps and who bears each cost.
  • Write complete draft problem frames that separate current conditions, a changed state, assumptions, and remaining unknowns.
  • Use AI to test and widen your frames while deciding what to accept or reject, then choose a problem that is workable for further inquiry.
  • Submit one problem frame that shows how your judgment changed, and explain what AI contributed, what only you could contribute, and what remains uncertain.
Complete Sprint 0, then follow Sprint 1's required items in order. Bring observations from your real work or life and use one Candidate Log throughout. Complete the brainstorm, table, and first frames without AI before using AI in the Dojo Lab.
