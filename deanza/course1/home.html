Sprint 1: Find the Problem Worth Solving (V2)First half V2▸
Module Learning Goals
  • Use observations, work-arounds, and requests from your own work or life to uncover gaps worth investigating.
  • Describe three to five candidate problems from your working table, naming who pays the cost and marking what you do not yet know.
  • Compare candidates using four checks: real people, current handling, current occurrence, and a size that supports further inquiry.
  • Explain your choice of a problem with reasons and a runner-up, or identify where to look next if nothing survives.
Only the first half of Sprint 1 is available here, ending with Test and commit. The Introduction also describes second-half work that is not yet available. Start with your own observations; you do not need a chosen problem. Keep one table across these activities and carry your choice into later framing. Work without AI in this half. The three own-your-progress activities are encouraged, carry zero points, and do not block later activities if skipped.
