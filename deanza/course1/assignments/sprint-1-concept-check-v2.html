You write, “I dread the weekly handover.” What does that give you at this stage?

  • A confirmed cause of the problem
  • An indicator to investigate by describing what happens
  • A solution to implement immediately

Which sentence belongs in the table’s current-state column?

  • We should buy a handover app.
  • Nobody cares about communicating.
  • The outgoing owner tells the incoming owner; the rest of the team hears about the change later.

You correct the same exported report by hand every month and no longer think of it as a problem. What is the useful next move?

  • Notice the routine and describe how it works and what gap it hides.
  • Discard it because familiar routines cannot be problems.
  • Assume everyone needs the same new software.

Someone says, “We need an app to track onboarding.” Where do you begin?

  • Choose features for the app.
  • Ask why the request matters until the goal becomes clear.
  • Treat the request itself as proof of the problem.

In the onboarding example, which answer has reached the goal?

  • Build a dashboard.
  • Give managers a more visible tracker.
  • New hires should contribute to their teams as quickly as possible.

After finding the goal behind a request, what do you do next?

  • List possible obstacles, then describe the ones you can observe as candidate problems.
  • Assume the original requested solution is the only path.
  • Ask AI to choose the problem for you.
