Sprint 4 Reflection: What I Learned and How I Know

Purpose

Say precisely what you can now do that you couldn’t two weeks ago, and be honest about which of your new confidence is earned.

Task Instructions

  1. Review your gap list, your learning path, your verification log, and your revised Goal Plan.
  2. Compare what you set out to learn with what you actually ended up learning.
  3. Write an initial reflection in the activity.
  4. Generate AI follow-up questions and use them to find what is still vague, overclaimed, or unchecked.
  5. Revise your reflection so that what you know is separated from what you were told.
  6. Use the deeper AI guidance button if your answer describes a topic rather than a capability.
  7. Save the refined response in the activity.

Deliverable

Your refined reflection should answer:

  • One thing you can now do or decide that you could not at the start of the sprint. A capability, not a topic.
  • What AI explained well, and whether you ever checked it.
  • What you checked, and what came back different from what you expected. If nothing came back different, what that tells you about the check.
  • What you’re still not ready to claim. Not what you haven’t learned yet, but something you half-know and would be tempted to state confidently in a meeting.
  • Whether either of the things you set aside now looks like a mistake.

Submission Format

Download the JSON response file from the activity and upload it to Canvas.

Evidence Of Success

Your final response should make it possible for someone else to tell which of your claims rest on evidence and which rest on a good explanation. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.
