Brainstorm Your List AI-guided activity

Overview

Start with what happened in your own week. AI cannot see what you kept working around, put off, or stopped noticing.

Make your list first

Take about ten minutes. Add 12 to 20 situations from your own week under at least two headings. Work, home, and other are enough. Add a heading such as job search, volunteering, caregiving, or side business if it better fits your week.

Write a short phrase for each situation. Keep the list broad. Do not decide whether an item counts as a problem, explain it, compare it, or narrow it down yet.

Walk through your week in order. Your calendar, to-do list, and inbox can help you recall situations such as:

  • Things you do over and over.
  • Things you dread, complain about, or wish worked differently.
  • A step you keep repairing because the usual process falls short.
  • Something you have been putting off.
  • A request that already names a solution, such as “we need an app,” before the underlying goal is clear.

Illustrative example

Work

  • Account handovers are easy to miss
  • Report columns are reformatted by hand each month
  • New people wait for access they need

Other

  • Volunteer shift sign-ups arrive across several group texts
  • My resume has not been updated in two years

Nothing on this list is a confirmed problem yet. Its job is to give you material to examine next.

Think this through with AI, if useful

After your list exists, you can open Think this through with AI. It may ask one or two questions to help you notice an area of your week that is thin or a workaround that has become normal.

The AI does not add items, decide what matters, or choose candidates. Answer only with details you are comfortable sharing. Do not include confidential workplace, client, or personal information. Add any new item to your own list in your own words. You can skip this panel and continue with the list you already made.

Copy your list to Canvas

Review that you have at least two headings and 12 to 20 specific situations from your own week. Keep the list broad. Then select Copy all. The copied text includes your list, not the AI conversation. Paste it into the Canvas text-entry box for this activity and submit there if you want to record completion.

Next, use three to five items from your list to describe how each situation works now and who bears its cost.
