AI Exchange: Is This a Gap?

Purpose

Find out whether what you committed to is really a gap, while you still have time to change it.

This is a test of work you have already done, not new work. Bring what you wrote in Name the Gap.

How It Works

  1. Paste in your committed gap, the decision waiting on it, and your definition of what “closed” looks like.
  2. The AI runs three tests on it and gives you a verdict on each.
  3. Revise anything that failed.
  4. Write two or three sentences on what it pushed on and what you did about it, including anything you decided it was wrong about.
  5. Save the conversation and upload it.

The Three Tests

  1. Can it finish the sentence? “Until I know this, I can’t ___.” If the AI cannot fill the blank from what you wrote, it will say what is missing.
  2. Is “closed” observable? Or is it a feeling. It will name what would make it pointable.
  3. Is it load-bearing? What would you have to do differently tomorrow if the answer came back the opposite way. If nothing changes, this is not the gap.

A Note on Arguing Back

This AI has been instructed to name a specific failure before it says anything encouraging, and not to accept a revision that only adds words. A gap does not become load-bearing by being described at greater length.

It has also been instructed to give you a verdict rather than general feedback. That does not make it right. If you think it has misread your situation, say so in your write-up and explain why. Disagreeing with it, in writing, with a reason, is a good answer.

What to Submit

The saved conversation, plus your two or three sentences on what changed.
