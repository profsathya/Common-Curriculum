# Sprint 3 Exam · What to prepare

Wednesday 2 December · In class · 200 points · Closed-book written exam and partner conversation

The Sprint 3 Exam asks you to evaluate your own report before your quality assurance partner does. Evaluating the quality of your own work is a skill that will remain valuable and be noticed throughout your career.

The exam has two halves of 100 points each. First you write your answers to two questions on paper, without your report in front of you. Then you and an exam partner, a classmate you pair up with in class that day, read each other's answers and ask each other questions about them. [for Sathya's read]

## Purpose

**Builds on your earlier learning.** The report you wrote in [My Sprint 3 report and winter break plan](my-sprint-3-report.html), and the updated quality assurance plan in its task 3. [for Sathya's read]

**This Learning Experience.** Evaluate your own report with one line of your quality assurance plan, and check that your quality assurance partner can answer that line from your report. [for Sathya's read]

**Prepare you for.** After the exam, your quality assurance partner gives you feedback on your report using the same plan, in the next graded item, [Grade your partners](grade-your-partners.html).

## What the written half asks

Both questions are about one line of your updated quality assurance plan: one of the "Do you think" questions you wrote for your quality assurance partner, the classmate you chose in My Sprint 3 goal and quality assurance plan, in task 3 of My Sprint 3 report. You choose the line.

**Question 1 is about the line (40 points).** Can your quality assurance partner answer it?

- Write the line you chose: your "Do you think" question and its follow-up question, as closely as you remember them.
- Describe what your quality assurance partner will look at in your report to answer it, such as a task, a sentence or a piece of evidence.
- Explain whether your quality assurance partner can answer it from your report alone. If something is missing or not clear in your line that could trip up your quality assurance partner, identify what is missing or not clear and rewrite the line.

**Question 2 is about your report (60 points).** What is your own answer to your line?

- Apply your own "Do you think" question to your own report to evaluate it: yes, partly or not yet.
- Give the reason for your answer, referring to what is in your report as much as possible from memory.
- Answer your follow-up question yourself, and describe one thing you will do because of your answers.

## How to prepare [for Sathya's read]

- Choose the line you will take into the exam, and find what your quality assurance partner will look at in your report to answer it. If you cannot find it, revise the line in task 3 or add to your report before you submit. If you have already submitted your report, you can rewrite the line in question 1 on the exam.
- Reread your submitted report before the exam. You will not have it in front of you, so know your line, its follow-up question and what your quality assurance partner will look at.
- Practice on paper without your report: write the line, give your own answer (yes, partly or not yet) with your reason, and answer your follow-up question.

*The + reads:* Examples

**Three examples, to shape your thinking — not models to copy**

Question 1 · A line my quality assurance partner can answer from my report:

- **The line** — Do you think the evidence in task 1 shows what my step showed me about the demo? — What would make it clearer?
- **What my quality assurance partner will look at** — task 1, where I give the two dates I gave the demo, who listened, and the question two of my three listeners asked.

Question 1 · A line I revised, because my quality assurance partner could not answer it from my report:

- **My first line** — Do you think two people who work with data would learn something from my demo once the new section is in?
- **What my quality assurance partner will look at** — nothing in my report shows the new section, because I have not written it yet. My quality assurance partner would have to guess.
- **My revised line** — Do you think the section I plan to add, described in task 2a, answers the question two of my listeners asked in task 1? — What else should the section show?

Question 2 · My own answer to one of my lines: [for Sathya's read]

- **The line** — Do you think my first step is both small enough to take before 18 December and meaningful enough to move my winter break goal forward? — How could I make it more meaningful?
- **My own answer** — Partly.
- **My reason** — In task 2c my first step is to write down where each column of my data comes from, in one page. It is small enough: it is one page, and I already have the data. I am less sure it is meaningful, because I wrote the page for myself, and my report does not say how it will reach the two listeners who asked about the data.
- **My answer to my follow-up question, and what I will do** — The step would be more meaningful if one of those listeners read the page. I will send it to my cousin by 18 December and ask whether it answers her question.

## The partner half [for Sathya's read]

After you write, you and an exam partner exchange papers. You type your partner's answers, ask at least two follow-up questions about what they wrote, and write a summary of what you learned from the conversation. A follow-up question teaches you more when it points at one specific thing in your partner's answer.

## After the exam [for Sathya's read]

- By Monday 7 December, give feedback on the report of each classmate who chose you as their quality assurance partner, in [Grade your partners](grade-your-partners.html). Your quality assurance partner does the same for your report.
