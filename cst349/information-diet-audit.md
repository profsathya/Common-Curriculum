CST349 · Fall 2026 · Sprint 3 · Week 11 · Set your goal

# Information Diet Audit

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Friday 13 November

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Learn to see what you actually pay attention to each week, so that you can choose one source on purpose for the Sprint 3 goal you just set. This activity is an audit of your real information diet, followed by one change to it.

☐ **I would like to use the AI Dojo to think this through.** With this ticked, the Dojo steps appear under the tasks. (Page note: unticked when the page opens, as on the other optional activities; remembered on this browser.)

## Part 1 · The audit

Document what you actually consumed over the past week, not what you wish you had consumed. Be specific.

**For each source or platform you regularly get information from:**

- What is it? Name, platform, type.
- How often do you check it? Daily, a few times a week, when bored.
- What kind of information does it give you? News, opinions, tutorials, entertainment, other people's takes.
- Did you choose it deliberately, or did it become a habit?

   **My sources.** One source per line, with the four answers.

**Then answer:**

- What patterns do you notice? Are most of your sources the same type, the same platform, the same perspective?
- What is missing? Which topics, viewpoints or kinds of source are absent?
- What does this tell you about what you have been optimizing for: understanding, or engagement?

   **What I notice.** The patterns, what is missing, and what I have been optimizing for.

## Part 2 · One change, for my Sprint 3 goal

Choose one new source that would help the Sprint 3 goal you set this week, one you have never used before. A person counts: an alum from the virtual visit on 18 November, someone at your job, a professor. A program like GOLDI counts, and so does a newsletter, a community, or a feed you have avoided. Write what it is, why it fits your goal, how often you will use it between now and 30 November, and what you will stop or reduce to make room for it.

   **My one change.** The source, why it fits my goal, how often, and what it replaces.
