# My Sprint 3 report: what I did against my own terms, and what comes next

Week 14 · due Monday 30 November, 11:59 p.m. · 100 points · Graded items

## Purpose (page note: three-box arrow chain, Builds on → This activity → Leads to)

**Builds on.** You set a Sprint 3 goal with the terms you want your work evaluated (graded) on, and two classmates agreed to hold you to them ([My Sprint 3 plan](my-sprint-3-plan.html), [My two graders](my-two-graders.html)).

**This activity.** Learn to report on your own work against terms you set yourself, with evidence a reader can check and a plain statement of what you did not reach.

**Leads to.** Send this report to your two graders the day you submit it, so they can evaluate (grade) it by 7 December, and bring it to the Sprint 3 Exam on Wednesday 2 December, where you reflect on this sprint in writing and with a partner.

Copy the three or four grading lines from your Sprint 3 plan, and under each one write what you did and include the evidence a reader can check. *(Your two graders go line by line, so give each line something checkable: a date, a name, a link, a number, a screenshot described in words. Where you did not meet a line, write that you did not meet it and what got in the way; that is a complete answer, and the self-check gives it points.)*

*The + reads:* An example, more examples, and a question

**An example, to shape your thinking — not a model to copy**

- **Line 1 · I gave the demo to three people outside my major, and my report names them and the dates · 30 pts** — Done. A classmate from statistics on 10 Nov, a coworker on 12 Nov, and my cousin who works in HR on 21 Nov; each one is in my calendar and I have their questions in my notes doc (link).
- **Line 3 · I changed one thing in the demo because of a question · 25 pts** — Partly. Two of the three asked where the data comes from, so I added a slide on that before the third demo. My line said I would change the demo itself, and a slide is a smaller change than that, so I am reporting it as partly met.

**More examples** (page note: a click-to-open inside the +, closed by default)

- **Line 2 · I showed the chart to my supervisor and my report records what she said · 30 pts** — Done on 13 Nov. Her words, from my notes: "Show me what happens in the two weeks after a password reset." The chart is in my work drive; I have described it here because the drive is not shareable.
- **Line 3 · My report shows the change I made and the time after · 35 pts** — Not met. The full file never loaded, because the date column failed again on rows after 400,000. I have the hundred-row table and the slow query on it (1.8 s on 100 rows, which tells me the index is the problem). What got in the way was that I did not read the column notes before I wrote the table, which is the same thing my first step showed me.

**Reflection (behind the +).** Which of your lines turned out to be a different promise than the work needed, and what would you write in its place now?
