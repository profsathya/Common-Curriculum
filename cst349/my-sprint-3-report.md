**An example, to shape your thinking — not a model to copy**

- **Line 1 · I gave the demo to three people outside my major, and my report names them and the dates · 30 pts** — Done. A classmate from statistics on 10 Nov, a coworker on 12 Nov, and my cousin who works in HR on 21 Nov; each one is in my calendar and I have their questions in my notes doc (link).
- **Line 3 · I changed one thing in the demo because of a question · 25 pts** — Partly. Two of the three asked where the data comes from, so I added a slide on that before the third demo. I did not change the demo itself, which is what my line said; I think the slide is the honest version of this.

**More examples** (page note: a click-to-open inside the +, closed by default)

- **Line 2 · I showed the chart to my supervisor and my report records what she said · 30 pts** — Done on 13 Nov. Her words, from my notes: "Show me what happens in the two weeks after a password reset." The chart is in my work drive; I have described it here because the drive is not shareable.
- **Line 3 · My report shows the change I made and the time after · 35 pts** — Not met. The full file never loaded; the date column failed again on rows after 400,000. I have the hundred-row table and the slow query on it (1.8 s on 100 rows, which tells me the index is the problem). What got in the way was not reading the column notes before I wrote the table, which my first step already warned me about.

**Reflection (behind the +).** Which of your lines turned out to be the wrong thing to have promised, and what would you promise instead?
