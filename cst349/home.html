CST349 · Extra credit · Being designed now

Look-back assignments · Earn back up to half of the points you lost

We are building a set of look-back assignments. Each one takes you back to an earlier assignment, so that you can learn what you missed the first time and earn back half of the points you have lost so far on all assignments and exams. The details are still being worked out, and the assignments will be posted on this tab over the next week.

What we are planning
  • There will be three to five look-back assignments, each on a different earlier assignment.
  • Each look-back is a guided conversation with your AI Dojo, run like an oral exam, and it takes about 30–40 minutes.
  • The conversation explores the topic of that assignment and how you approached your learning in it.
  • You submit the full conversation, and we review it as a whole. The look-backs either earn back half of your lost points or they do not; there is no partial credit.
What you can do now
  • Keep your earlier submissions where you can find them. The conversations will ask you to bring in what you wrote.
