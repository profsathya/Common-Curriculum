# How should Sprint 3 work

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Friday 30 October

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Sprint 3 is about meeting the people and the work behind the field you are preparing for. Choose how you would like the class to do it, so that Sprint 3 is built around what you will actually use.

## The options

Read the three, and the fourth is yours.

1. **The class organizes a virtual networking event together.** One event for the whole class, with industry guests we invite, where every student has a role in making it happen and a conversation in it.

2. **Small groups each organize one.** Each guide group plans and runs its own small virtual event with one or two guests of its choosing, and the groups share what they learned.

3. **The dream-conference trail.** Pick the tech conference you would attend if money were no object. Find two or three of its talks, and the people and the projects behind them, and follow that trail as far as it goes.

4. **Something else.** Say what.

## Task

Choose one, and say why in a few sentences: what you would get out of it that the others would not give you.

   **My choice, and why.** The option by number, or my own, and the reason.
