# Sprint 2, week by week: what you do, and what you have at the end of each week

Each week builds on the one before. By the exam, you have reflected on your value proposition (ALan -> this should be hover text) as a competitive candidate and developed a plan to build evidence to support that value proposition.

## The five weeks

### Week 7 · Oct 5 – 11 — Find the question that will anchor your growth

- **You do:** Use behavioral interview questions to reflect deeply on your readiness, and select a difficult question as starting point to get the most benefit of the reflection. Explore it in detail through online chat with three classmates.
- **You have at the end:** A better understanding of the most useful areas of growth you should focus on.
- **Graded:** Plan your professional preparation intentionally · Mon Oct 12

### Week 8 · Oct 12 – 18 — Set your goal

- **You do:** Imagine an initial answer that would position you well, using the [[STAR method|Situation, Task, Action, Result: a way to tell the story of an experience so an interviewer can see what you did and what came of it.]], and use it to develop a plan of action.
- **You have at the end:** A personalized plan of action that will help you prepare a quality [[value proposition|What you offer an employer, presented clearly: the skills and experiences you bring, the evidence for your claims and the value you bring to their team and business.]] for someone to hire you.
- **Graded:** My Sprint 2 goal · Fri Oct 23

### Week 9 · Oct 19 – 25 — Practice the answer

- **You do:** Mock interview practice: Presenting your answer aloud to a classmate who pushes back with the follow-up questions an interviewer would ask, so you can make your answer stronger and improve your ability to provide such answers.
- **You have at the end:** A clear understanding of the gap between the answer you want to give and the one you can give today. Use that understanding to deelop a plan of action.
- **Graded:** What the practice showed · Mon Oct 26

### Week 10 · Oct 26 – Nov 1 — Take the first step

- **You do:** Take at least the first real step from your plan.
- **You have at the end:** An initial piece of real evidence supporting your value proposition. (Even if it is a draft and need more improvement, that is ok).
- **Graded:** My first step, taken · Mon Nov 2

### Week 11 · Nov 2 – 8 — Show what you learned

- **You do:** Look back on how you moved from a question you could not answer to a plan you are acting on, and explain it in writing and in conversation.
- **You have at the end:** A process for preparing that you can repeat to continuously refine your value proposition and build evidence to support it.
- **Graded:** Sprint 2 Exam · Wed Nov 4

## What carries forward from week to week

1. The **question** that shows where you can grow most
2. The **answer** you are aiming for, and a **plan** that gets you there
3. The **gap** a mock interview practice helped you identify
4. The first **evidence** that puts your plan into action
5. **Taking ownership** of your own process to adapt how you prepare to meet what the industry

## After the exam · optional · Fri Nov 6 — Talk your plan through with a professional

- **You do:** Test your plan by talking to someone in the industry. If you feel you are not ready for that, you can meet with Jeff D'Andria to have your resume reviewed.
- **You have at the end:** Experience networking with an industry professional and incorporating their feedback into your your own process.
