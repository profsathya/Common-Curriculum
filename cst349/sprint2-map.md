# Sprint 2, week by week: what you do, and what you have at the end of each week

Each week builds on the one before. By the exam, a question you could not answer well has become an answer you are working toward, a plan you are acting on, and the first real experience behind it.

## The five weeks

### Week 7 · Oct 5 – 11 — Find the question that will anchor your growth

- **You do:** Use behavioral interview questions to challenge your readiness, and focus on the hardest question to get the most benefit. Explore it in detail through online chat with three classmates.
- **You have at the end:** A better understanding of the most useful areas of growth you can focus on, and one particular question to use as your anchor.
- **Graded:** Plan your professional preparation intentionally · Mon Oct 12

### Week 8 · Oct 12 – 18 — Set your goal

- **You do:** Imagine an initial answer that would position you well, using the [[STAR method|Situation, Task, Action, Result: a way to tell the story of an experience so an interviewer can see what you did and what came of it.]], and use it to develop a plan of action.
- **You have at the end:** A personalized plan of action that will help you prepare a quality [[value proposition|What you offer an employer, said clearly: the skills and experiences you bring, and the kind of problems you can help them with.]] for someone to hire you.
- **Graded:** My Sprint 2 goal · Mon Oct 19

### Week 9 · Oct 19 – 25 — Practice the answer

- **You do:** Say your answer aloud to a classmate who pushes back with the follow-up questions an interviewer would ask, so you find where your story is thin before an interviewer does.
- **You have at the end:** A clear view of the gap between the answer you want to give and the one you can give today, and the experience worth building next.
- **Graded:** What the practice showed · Mon Oct 26

### Week 10 · Oct 26 – Nov 1 — Take the first step

- **You do:** Take the first real step from your plan, and look at what it produced.
- **You have at the end:** The first piece of real evidence behind your answer: an experience you can speak about because it happened, and a sharper next step.
- **Graded:** My first step, taken · Mon Nov 2

### Week 11 · Nov 2 – 8 — Show what you learned

- **You do:** Look back on how you moved from a question you could not answer to a plan you are acting on, and explain it in writing and in conversation.
- **You have at the end:** A way of preparing that you can repeat for any question an interviewer could ask, and the evidence that you have used it once.
- **Graded:** Sprint 2 Exam · Wed Nov 4

## What carries forward from week to week

1. The **question** that shows where you can grow most
2. The **answer** you are aiming for, and the **plan** that gets you there
3. The **gap** a real listener helped you see
4. The first **evidence** that the answer is becoming real
5. A **way of preparing** you can use for any question

## After the exam · optional · Fri Nov 6 — Talk your plan through with a professional

- **You do:** Put your plan in front of a professional who does the work you are heading toward, and ask them what you are missing. Or ask Jeff D'Andria to review your resume.
- **You have at the end:** A plan checked against how the industry hires, and a first professional contact who knows what you are working toward.

Side note: This is where the plan leaves the classroom. **The message you write is the one the mentor reads**, so what you built in weeks 7 to 10 is what you have to offer them.
