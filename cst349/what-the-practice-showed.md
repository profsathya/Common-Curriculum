# What the practice showed

Graded item · Due Date: Monday 26 October · 100 pts

This graded item will affect your final grade, and the late penalty applies.

## Purpose

Use what your partner's questions exposed on Wednesday to see what your answer still needs, so that the next experience you build is the one that fills the gap.

☑ **I would like to use the AI Dojo to think this through.** With this ticked, the Dojo steps appear under the tasks. Untick it to work on your own with the AI feedback button under each box. (Page note: ticked when the page opens; remembered on this browser.)

**Before you start with the AI Dojo:** this route works only if the course Google Doc is linked in your AI Dojo project. Check that it is linked, so that your Dojo works from the latest information about the course.

## Tasks

1. Open your mock-interview transcript on The Commons and read your own answer as you gave it. Write it here as you said it, not as you prepared it. If it is imagined, say so.

   **The answer I gave.** Situation, task, action, result, as they came out on Wednesday.

2. Write the follow-up questions your partner asked and what you said to each. Then write what your partner told you afterwards.

   **My partner's questions and my replies.** Each question, and what I said.

   **My partner's feedback.** What they said the answer showed, and what it did not.

3. Name the gap. Where did the answer get thin: a part of the experience you have not had yet, a detail you could not give, or a result you could not state? One gap, the one that matters most.

   *With your Dojo:* tell your Dojo the questions you could not answer well, and let it ask until the gap is one sentence.

   **The gap the practice exposed.** One sentence, and why it is the one that matters.

4. Go back to Part 4 of My Sprint 2 goal. Write one line about the step you said you would take that week: taken or not, and what happened. If the gap in task 3 changes what the next step should be, say what the next step is now.

   **My first step, one line.** Taken or not, what happened, and what the next step is now.
