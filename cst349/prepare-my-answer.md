# Prepare my answer

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Wednesday 21 October, before class

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Come to Wednesday's mock interview with an answer you have already said out loud once, so that the practice in class goes to the follow-up questions and not to finding your words.

☑ **I would like to use the AI Dojo to prepare my answer.** With this ticked, your Dojo plays the interviewer under the tasks below. Untick it to prepare on your own with the AI feedback button under the answer. (Page note: ticked when the page opens; remembered on this browser.)

**Before you start with the AI Dojo:** this route works only if the course Google Doc is linked in your AI Dojo project. Check that it is linked, so that your Dojo works from the latest information about the course.

## Tasks

1. Copy your question from Part 1 of My Sprint 2 goal, and write your STAR answer to it the way you will say it on Wednesday: situation, task, action, result, in about one minute of speaking. Use the answer from your goal form as the starting point, and tighten it.

   **My question.** The question, in full.

   **My answer, as I will say it.** Situation, task, action, result, in the words I will use aloud.

   ☐ **This answer is imagined.** On Wednesday you tell your partner this before you start, so that their questions help you plan the experience rather than test a memory.

2. Say the answer out loud once, by yourself, and time it. Then write the two follow-up questions an interviewer would most likely ask after hearing it: the place where your answer is thin, and the place where it is interesting.

   *With your Dojo:* paste the prompt below, give your answer, and let your Dojo ask the follow-up questions. Write the two it asked that you found hardest.

   **The two follow-up questions I expect.** The question, and what I would say.
