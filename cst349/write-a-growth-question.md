# Write a question that will test your growth from Sprint 1

## Purpose
Prepare for the Sprint 1 Exam by writing a question that tests your growth and trying it with a partner.

- **Write one exam question about your Sprint 1 goal.** Choose a question that lets you demonstrate your growth.
- **Outline what a good answer would include.** Use the evidence you set for yourself in week 3 and explain what it shows now.
- **Explain what your answer demonstrates.** How does the evidence show growth, beyond the effort you put in?
- **Try your question with your partner.** Ask them your question, then discuss your answer outline together. Ask your partner to pose a follow-up question about your evidence, and write down that question.

## Submit
Include your exam question, answer outline, explanation of the growth it shows, and your partner’s follow-up question.

You can submit your own summary, an AI Dojo chat log, or another format that includes these four parts. Paste your submission into the Canvas text box.
