Sprint 2, week by week: what you do, and what you have at the end of each week

Each week produces one thing, and the next week starts from it. By the exam, you have taken a question you could not answer well and turned it into an answer, a plan, and a first real experience.

Week 7 · Oct 5 – 11
Find the question that will anchor your growth
You do

Choose the behavioral interview question that is hardest for you today, post it, and chat with three classmates about it.

You have at the end

One question, with your reason it is hard, tested in three conversations.

Graded: Plan your professional preparation intentionally · Mon Oct 12
Week 8 · Oct 12 – 18
Set your goal
You do

Write the answer you want to be able to give, in STAR form, real or imagined. Work backwards from it to six months, three months, and this week.

You have at the end

The answer you are aiming for, and a plan with a first step dated this week.

Graded: My Sprint 2 goal · Mon Oct 19
Week 9 · Oct 19 – 25
Practice the answer
You do

Give your answer aloud to a classmate who asks the follow-up questions an interviewer would ask, then interview them.

You have at the end

The place where your answer is thin, and which experience to build next.

Graded: What the practice showed · Mon Oct 26
Week 10 · Oct 26 – Nov 1
Take the first step
You do

Take the first step from your plan. Write what you did, what it produced, and what the next step is now.

You have at the end

One real experience you can talk about, and the next step with a date.

Graded: My first step, taken · Mon Nov 2
Week 11 · Nov 2 – 8
Show what you learned
You do

In the exam, explain how you went from the question to the answer, the plan, and the first step, in writing and in conversation.

You have at the end

A process you can repeat for any question an interviewer could ask.

Graded: Sprint 2 Exam · Wed Nov 4

What carries forward from week to week

  1. The question you cannot answer well today
  2. The answer you want to give, and the plan that makes it real
  3. The gap a real listener found in the answer
  4. The experience your first step produced
  5. The process you followed, which you can now reuse
After the exam · optional · Fri Nov 6
Talk your plan through with a professional

Write a message to a mentor in Prof. Sathya's network asking for 30 minutes on your plan, or ask Jeff D'Andria for a 30-minute resume review.

You have at the end

Your plan checked by someone who does the work you are preparing for.

This is where the plan leaves the classroom. The message you write is the one the mentor reads, so everything you built in weeks 7 to 10 is what you have to say.
