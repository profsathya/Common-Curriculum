# My first step, taken

Graded item · Due Date: Monday 2 November · 100 pts

This graded item will affect your final grade, and the late penalty applies.

## Purpose

Turn your plan into the first experience you can talk about, so that the answer you wrote in week 8 starts becoming real rather than staying a plan.

☑ **I would like to use the AI Dojo to think this through.** With this ticked, the Dojo steps appear under the tasks. Untick it to work on your own with the AI feedback button under each box. (Page note: ticked when the page opens; remembered on this browser.)

**Before you start with the AI Dojo:** this route works only if the course Google Doc is linked in your AI Dojo project. Check that it is linked, so that your Dojo works from the latest information about the course.

## Tasks

1. Write what you did. The step as you took it, with the day, the person or the thing involved, and what you had to do to make it happen. If the step you took is not the one you planned in week 8, say what changed and why.

   **What I did.** The step, the day, who or what it involved, and what it took.

2. Write what it produced. Something another person could see: a reply, a meeting that happened, a file, a measurement, a first version. If it produced less than you expected, write what it did produce.

   **What it produced.** The thing someone else could see, and where it is.

3. Write the next step. Look at your 3-month box from the goal form and at what this step produced. What is the next step now, with a day?

   *With your Dojo:* tell your Dojo what the step produced, and let it ask what has to be true next for the 3-month box to be real.

   **The next step now.** One step, with a day, and how it moves the 3-month box.

4. Write one line connecting this step to the answer you wrote in week 8: which part of the STAR answer got closer to real because of it.

   **What got closer to real.** Situation, task, action or result, and how.
