## The shared start

- **PARTIAL — Conversation as the opening move (teacher · peers · AI, in combinations).**
  Evidence: 349 adopts the 286 conversation mechanism wholesale (July — with Sathya's note that the mechanism itself "needs to be tested for the age of AI"), and the spring site already has `peer-conversations.html` as raw material.
  Missing: the opening arc, AI's seat in the conversations, and the rebuild.

- **NOT YET — Symbiotic Thinking, named and practiced.**
  Evidence: the definition exists.
  Missing: no course surface introduces it.

## The self-directed learning layer (on your own professional growth)

- **PARTIAL — The pursuit is the student's own professional becoming, declared openly.**
  Evidence: locked principle (July).
  Missing: the build under the new outcome.

- **PARTIAL — Define growth comprehensively before chasing it.**
  Evidence: locked — multi-dimensional with metrics, and `sdl-dimensions.html` from spring is the scaffold.
  Missing: the rebuild.

- **PARTIAL — Gather and demonstrate evidence.**
  Evidence: locked — definition, movement, and direction evidence; grade the loop, not the line; the weekly milestone loop is the follow-through spine and the student runs it.
  Missing: the big one — consolidating the ~60-assignment spring structure to ~8–12 (that consolidation *is* the design, decided July); then the build.

- **PARTIAL — Carry the learning somewhere new (transfer).**
  Evidence: the Sprint 3 transfer rep was adopted for 349 in July.
  Missing: what a second context means for a professional-growth pursuit, and the build.

## The integrating-perspectives layer — the layer 349 adds

- **NOT YET — Combine two or more disciplines, or stakeholder viewpoints, to find a new space of value (T-shaped toward comb-shaped).**
  Evidence: named in the 10 Aug framework.
  Missing: everything — none of 349's three locked principles carries it, no activity teaches it, and reconciling it with the locked design is open. This is the biggest design gap in the course.

## The two questions

- **NOT YET.** Same call as the other courses (10 Aug): plant them early, as the value-proposition frame. Missing: when, inside what activity, and the materials.

## The nurture-track thread (six qualities)

- **NOT YET — Every activity aligned to a named subset of the six qualities.**
  Evidence: principle decided 10–11 Aug — the qualities are woven throughout the course rather than treated as a start/end measure; each activity carries a subset chosen by its focus; Map Your Curiosity is out as a course activity; the opening Know-Yourself activity carries self-knowledge and probably self-regulation.
  Missing: the activity-by-activity mapping for this course.

- **NOT YET — Movement on those qualities watched per activity.**
  Evidence: decided 11 Aug — conversations with AI Sensei run throughout the course (the two-conversation week 1–2 / week 6–8 design is superseded; note on the 8/10 plan file).
  Missing: how each activity's conversations or artifacts record movement, and which parts of the plan file survive per conversation.

## The scaffolded introduction sequence

- **NOT YET.** Order sketched 10 Aug (orient → conversation + Symbiotic Thinking → the layers → the two questions); flagged as needing careful thinking; nothing designed.
