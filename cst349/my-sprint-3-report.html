Purpose

Builds on your earlier learning

Setting a learning goal, and applying lessons from diverse information sources to revise and strengthen it.

This Learning Experience

Build on that experience to adapt and update your plan for professional growth.

Prepare you for

Becoming a self-directed learner, taking charge of your own professional growth.

    Examples and Reflection
    An example, to shape your thinking — not a model to copy

    The step I took — I gave my CST 338 project demo to my second and third listeners: a coworker on 12 November and my cousin, who works in HR, on 21 November.

    What it showed me about my goal — two of my three listeners asked where the data comes from. The demo shows what the project does, but it does not yet show why someone should trust the result.

    Evidence — both dates are in my calendar, and their questions are in my notes doc (link).

    More examples

    The step I took — I made the chart of lockouts per department per week, but my supervisor was out until December, so I showed it to the senior technician on 18 November instead.

    What it showed me about my goal — he pointed out that two departments share one help-desk queue, so the chart counts some lockouts twice. I need to fix the data before the proposal makes sense.

    Evidence — the chart and his comment are in my notes doc (link); I described the method and shared only the aggregate counts I am allowed to share.

    The step I took — I tried to load the full airline file into PostgreSQL. It failed on the date column after row 400,000, so I timed the slowest query on a hundred-row table instead (1.8 s).

    What it showed me about my goal — I did not read the column notes before I wrote the table. Speeding up the query depends on first understanding the data.

    Evidence — the error message and the timing are in my notes doc (link).

    When the work is private. Use an excerpt, redacted output, aggregate result, or description of your method that you are allowed to share. Explain what it shows and what your classmate cannot verify from it.

    Reflection

    What did the step show you that planning it in October did not?
