## The ten questions

Behavioral interview questions ask you to describe something you actually did. Some variation of these ten are asked commonly. They are adapted from Indeed's career guide, [10 Behavioral Interview Questions (With Sample Answers)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/most-common-behavioral-interview-questions-and-answers). Note: I am not sure the sample answers are very good.

**Working with people**

1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone you were working with. What did you do?
2. Tell me about a time you helped a team do better work than it was doing.

**When things go wrong**

3. Tell me about a mistake you made. How did you correct it?
4. Tell me about a time you did not succeed at a task. What did you do next?
5. Tell me about a difficult decision you had to make. How did you make it?
6. Tell me about a time you had to organize your time to meet a deadline.

**Growing and taking initiative**

7. Tell me about a time you took on more than you were asked to do.
8. Tell me about a skill you had to learn while you were already doing the work.
9. Tell me about a goal you set for yourself and how you reached it.
10. Tell me about a problem you solved. Walk me through your steps.

   **Hardest question 2.** The question, and why it is hard for me.

   **Hardest question 3.** The question, and why it is hard for me.

3. Choose one of your three questions to discuss with classmates.

   **The question I will post.** The question, and why I chose this one to discuss.

4. Log in to [the-commons.symbioticthinking.ai](https://the-commons.symbioticthinking.ai) with your csumb.edu email address, and find the **The interview questions I find hardest** online chat activity in your dashboard. Post using this format: The question I find hardest is: ___. It is hard for me because: ___.

5. Open three classmates' posts and chat with each of them. Ask questions that help your classmate see why the question is hard for them: is the experience missing, or is it there and not yet thought through? Answer the questions you receive in the same way.

6. After your three chats, write what you now see about your own question.

   **What I learned from the chats.** What I now understand about why this question is hard for me, and one thing I could do in Sprint 2 to answer it better.
