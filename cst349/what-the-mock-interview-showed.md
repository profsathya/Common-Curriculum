# What the mock interview showed

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Wednesday 28 October

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Use what your partner's questions brought up in a mock interview to see how your answer can be improved.

## Your mock interview

You need one mock interview to answer the questions below. Use either one:

- **The mock interview in class on Wednesday 21 October.** Work from the notes you wrote down there.
- **A mock interview on Zoom with a classmate.** Set up about 20 minutes with a classmate. Present your answer to them in a mock interview role play, and ask your classmate to ask the follow-up questions an interviewer would ask. Write down their questions and your replies as you go. Then switch, and interview your classmate the same way.

**My mock interview.** In class or on Zoom, and the name of the classmate I partnered with.

☑ **I would like to use the AI Dojo to think this through.** With this ticked, the Dojo steps appear under the tasks. Untick it to work on your own with the AI feedback button under each box. (Page note: ticked when the page opens; remembered on this browser.)

**Before you start with the AI Dojo:** this route works only if the course Google Doc is linked in your AI Dojo project. Check that it is linked, so that your Dojo works from the latest information about the course.

## Tasks

1. Write your answer as you gave it in the mock interview. If the experience is imagined, say so.

   **The answer I gave.** Situation, task, action, result, as they came out in the mock interview.

2. Write the follow-up questions your partner asked and how you replied. Then summarize key ideas that came from your partner.

   **My partner's questions and my replies.** Each question, and what I said.

   **My partner's feedback.** Summary of their response.

3. Identify the gap. Where did your answer fall short: a part of the experience you have not had yet, a detail you could not give, or a result you could not state?

   *With your Dojo:* tell your Dojo the questions you could not answer well, and have it interview you until your answer is precise.

   **The gap the mock interview exposed.** What you learned about your own preparation.
