# Why a partner who questions you is worth having

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Wednesday 7 October · Margaret Heffernan, TED, 13 min

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Explore how a partner's questions can help you examine the reasoning behind your growth goal.

## Watch

Watch before your first online chat on The Commons: Margaret Heffernan, [Dare to disagree](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PY_kd46RfVE), TEDGlobal 2012, about 13 minutes.

Heffernan describes how Alice Stewart and George Kneale worked together to examine the evidence behind Stewart's research findings.

## Tasks

Write your answer to each task in the box under it. The first three tasks link to the relevant part of the talk.

1. Explain how Kneale's efforts "to prove Dr. Stewart wrong" helped Stewart assess her conclusion. What did he do to test her finding? [Watch this part, 3:55–5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PY_kd46RfVE&t=235s)

   **Your answer to task 1.** How Kneale tested the finding and how that helped Stewart assess it.

2. Explain what a classmate with different experience or a different approach could help you notice about your growth goal. Connect your answer to Heffernan's advice about choosing people to think with. [Watch this part, 5:30–6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PY_kd46RfVE&t=330s)

   **Your answer to task 2.** What a different perspective could help you notice about your goal.

3. Describe what happened after Joe raised his concern at work. How did his colleagues respond, and what did their response make possible? Use details from Heffernan's account. [Watch this part, 7:24–9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PY_kd46RfVE&t=444s)

   **Your answer to task 3.** The concern Joe raised, his colleagues' response, and what happened next.

4. Write one question a classmate could ask to help you examine an assumption behind your Sprint 2 growth goal. Explain what answering it would help you understand.

   **Your answer to task 4.** A question about an assumption behind your goal, and what answering it would help you understand.
