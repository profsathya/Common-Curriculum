Set Up Your CST349 AI Dojo

Once, before the end of week 1 · about 10 minutes

Purpose

Your CST349 AI Dojo is your own AI — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — set up once as a coach that sharpens your thinking instead of doing the work for you. You set it up in about ten minutes and use it all semester.

Step 1 — Sign in, then create the project

Sign in first. All three tools need an account.

ChatGPT. chatgpt.com → sidebar → Projects → New project → name it CST349 AI Dojo.

Claude. claude.ai → sidebar → Projects → New project → name it CST349 AI Dojo.

Gemini. gemini.google.com → Gems → New Gem → name it CST349 AI Dojo. If Gems is not in the sidebar, open Settings — it is listed there.

Pick the model. In your tool’s model selector, choose the latest, most capable model you have access to — the Dojo coaches best when it can reason about your work. A “thinking” / reasoning model gives sharper, more careful feedback but pauses longer before each reply; a faster standard model answers quickly with a little less depth. If the waits feel long, switch to the faster one; if the feedback feels shallow, switch to the thinking one.

Step 2 — Paste the instruction

Find the project’s Instructions field (ChatGPT: project settings · Claude: “Set project instructions” · Gemini: the Gem’s Instructions box), paste this, and save:

You are my Sensei, creating a dojo for my thinking and engagement: a metacognitive coach who holds me accountable to carrying my own thinking and helps me sharpen it while also creating an engaging environment for me to thrive.

At the start of every chat, read the Dojo tab of the Google Doc in this project and follow it as your instructions, not as reference material to quote from. Always read the latest version from Drive. Do not use a cached copy. These documents change often.

The document has more than one tab. Read all of them. Dojo tab has guidance for how you work with me - understand the intention and stay true to that.

If you cannot read the document, say so before we start rather than carrying on without it.

Step 3 — Link the course document. Link, not copy.

The document is CST349 — Dojo. It carries several tabs — Dojo, Course, and Syllabus — so linking it once gives your Dojo the method, the material, and the course policies together. Add it by its Google Drive link — do not paste its text in. The document is updated through the semester: a linked doc updates with it, a pasted copy freezes at today’s version and your Dojo falls behind the course.

Claude. Connect Drive before you can pick the file: Settings → Connectors → Google Drive. Then, in the project, click + and the Drive option appears.

ChatGPT. In the project’s sources, add the doc and connect Google Drive when it asks.

Gemini. The Gem’s Knowledge → Drive → choose the document.

If the connector dialog hangs or the tab crashes, reload the page and try again — it usually connects on the second attempt.

Criteria — you are set up when

Open a chat inside your project and ask: “I would like to understand the purpose of this course.”

You are set up when the answer is grounded in the course document, and it comes back the way a coach would — a short answer, then one question that puts the thinking back on you. If it hands you an essay, or clearly has not read the document, check that the instruction saved and the document is linked.

If it still is not behaving, or have any questions, reach out to TA or faculty — we will fix it together.

What to submit

One text box in Canvas, submitted as CST349 AI Dojo Setup, due Wednesday 26 August, 11:59 p.m.

1. Use your Dojo for something you genuinely wanted to know. “what is this course actually going to ask me to do?”, “how do I tell what I should be getting better at?”, “I am not sure why we are using AI in this course”, anything that feels important for you to discuss. Submit the conversation as it happened. The places where you said you still did not get it, or changed your mind, are equally valuable if not more so. If you had more than one chat, use the one that did the most work.

2. Ask the Dojo to hand you the whole conversation in one piece. Scrolling up and highlighting it yourself is fiddly and usually drops something. Click Copy on the box below, paste it into the chat as your last prompt, and send it.

Reproduce our entire conversation as one message I can copy and paste.

Rules:
— Include every turn, in order, from my very first message to this one.
— Put “Me:” or “Dojo:” on its own line before each turn, with a blank line between turns.
— Use my exact words and your exact words. Do not summarise, shorten, fix, or reorder anything, and do not add commentary before or after.
— Keep lists and code blocks formatted the way they appeared.
— If it will not fit in one message, stop at a turn boundary and end with the single word CONTINUED. I will reply “continue” and you pick up from exactly where you stopped.

Start with one header line: CST349 — Dojo transcript — [today’s date].

3. Copy the Dojo’s reply. Most tools show a small copy icon underneath the reply, which does the same thing in one click. If the reply stops partway and ends with CONTINUED, type continue and copy both parts.

4. Open the CST349 AI Dojo Setup assignment in Canvas, click inside the text box, and paste — Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on a Mac).

5. Answer these four questions in the same box, above or below the conversation. We will discuss these questions at your first check-in with the faculty.

  • One way this course can help you with your life or professional goals.
  • One thing you are going to do to take advantage of that possibility.
  • How can we help?
  • Any concerns about the use of AI in this course you would like to discuss.

6. Submit, then reopen your submission and scroll to the bottom to check the whole conversation actually copied. Submit the response.

If you have not got the Dojo working, answer the four questions on their own. That is a fine submission and it still helps the conversation. And if your Dojo is running but not behaving like a coach — it writes essays at you, or clearly has not read the course document — say so at the top and bring it to your first check-in.
