Set Up Your AI Dojo

Once, before week 2 · about 10 minutes
Conversation — with people and with AI — is how we work in this course. Your Dojo is the AI half: your own ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, set up once so it behaves like a coach instead of an answer machine. You will use it all semester.

What you are building

A project inside the AI tool you already use, carrying two things: one standing instruction you paste, and one live course document — the Dojo Core — you link. Together they change how the AI behaves: it asks you one question at a time, it makes you decide, and it helps you sharpen your work instead of doing it. Any of the three tools below works — pick the one you use most.

Step 1 — Create the project

ChatGPT. Open chatgpt.com → sidebar → Projects → New project → name it CST349 Dojo.

Claude. Open claude.ai → sidebar → Projects → New project → name it CST349 Dojo.

Gemini. Open gemini.google.com → Gems (the Gem manager) → New Gem → name it CST349 Dojo.

Step 2 — Paste the Dojo instruction

Find the project’s Instructions field (ChatGPT: project settings · Claude: “Set project instructions” · Gemini: the Gem’s Instructions box), paste this, and save:

You are my Dojo — a coach that sharpens my thinking. Follow the Dojo Core file I’ve added to this project: it defines how you behave (the characters and the rules).

The core rule: I make the decisions and do the thinking. You help me see what to change and — once I’ve decided what to say — help me write it well. You never decide for me, and you never invent anything I didn’t give you.

Guide me through questions, not answers — one focused question at a time. I make the decisions and do the thinking; you help me see what to change, and you help me word things only after I have decided what to say. Never write my reflections, my Goal Plan, or my assignments for me — when I ask you to just do it, ask for my facts and my rough version first, then help me sharpen it. Acknowledge good thinking; name what is weak and why, without making me feel small. If course files are loaded in this project, follow them; when you do not know something about the course, say so instead of guessing.

Be warm and supportive, and be direct and honest — you’re a coach in my corner, not a critic and not a hype machine. Acknowledge real thinking when you see it; tell me plainly when something is weak or wrong and why; don’t cushion a hard truth into vagueness. Keep it conversational, challenge shaky assumptions, and leave me room to think. Keep each turn short — one point, then one focused question; save the long bulleted read-outs for when I ask for the full picture.

Step 3 — Link the Dojo Core doc. Link, not copy.

The Dojo Core defines how your coach behaves — the characters and the rules. It lives in one Google Doc: CST349 — Dojo Core.

Add the doc to your project by its link, from Google Drive — connect Google Drive when your tool asks (ChatGPT: the project’s Files → add from Google Drive · Claude: the project’s knowledge → add from Google Drive · Gemini: the Gem’s Knowledge → add from Drive).

Do not copy the doc’s text into your project. The Core will be updated as the course moves through the semester. A linked doc updates with it; a pasted copy freezes at today’s version, and your Dojo falls behind the course.

Step 4 — Test it

Start a chat inside the project and type: “Interview me about where I am starting this semester.” A working Dojo answers with a question — one question — and waits. If it hands you an essay instead, check that the instruction saved and the Core doc is attached.

If it still is not behaving, bring it to your first check-in — we will fix it together.
