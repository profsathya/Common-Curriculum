Talk your plan through with a professional

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Friday 6 November
Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

Purpose

Experience the networking step of connecting and verifying your current plan with a professional in the industry and incorporating their feedback into your approach.

How this works

You write the message to the mentor yourself - have it rigorously tested by your peers and by a frontier AI model. Then, I read it, and if it is a message I feel is supported by evidence and feel comfortable sending it out, I send it to 1-3 people you chose from my network. While I can't guarantee that we will get a response, the aim is to get a 30 min call for you with an industry mentor.

The mentors are listed by role and field, here. You will learn who they are when I connect you.

Before you start with the AI Dojo: this route works only if the course Google Doc is linked in your AI Dojo project. Check that it is linked, so that your Dojo works from the latest information about the course.

  5. Paste it into the message box under task 2, fix anything marked [not yet backed], and then go through the Self-check.

Tasks

  1. 1.

    Choose who you would like to talk to. Open the mentor list and pick one to three rows, in order of preference. Choose by the work the person does, and whether it is the work your plan is heading toward.

    Mentor list

    The row numbers in order of preference, and the role as the list gives it for each.

    The Google Doc link, with sharing set so that anyone with the link can comment.

  2. 2.

    Write the message to your mentor. Write it to the person, not to me. Use these parts, each a short paragraph, so that the whole message fits on one screen:

    • Who you are: your year, your track, and the kind of work you are interested in.
    • Explain your current plan of action to strengthen your value proposition with why you think this is a good plan for your unique situation.
    • One or two specific questions you would like them to comment on about your plan.
    • The ask: 30 minutes on Zoom, with 2-3 times you are free in the next three weeks. More options you provide makes it more likely that someone will talk to you.

    The whole message, ready to send.

  3. 3.

    Test the message before you submit it. Give it to a classmate in your guide group and ask them to read it as the mentor would: what would they still need to ask you before they could help? Then use the AI feedback button under the message, or your AI Dojo, and revise until every claim about what you have done points to something you actually did.

  4. 4.

    If you ticked the resume review, write one line for Jeff.

    The kind of role the resume is for, and the one thing you most want him to look at.

Self-check

Tick each line your message meets. Your ticks are copied with your message.

What makes it strong. The mentor can tell from the message what you have already done and what you are asking them to look at, without needing to ask you a first question.
