Week 4 — Begin Own Your SearchOwn Your Search▸
Week's Learning Goals
  • Mindset shift: see your job search as something to learn from, not just get through.
  • Workflow shift: build a workflow that turns each week into learning: a bet, a fair test, a read, and an adjustment.
  • The payoff: when you search to learn, you get better fast, and that’s what puts you in the path of good opportunities.
Builds on: your verified niche and sharpened resume from Week 3. You can only test a search once you know what you’re aiming at.
