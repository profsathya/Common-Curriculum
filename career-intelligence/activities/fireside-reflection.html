Career Intelligence · Week 5 · Own Your Search

Fireside Reflection

This activity's goal

Turn what the recruiters said into one concrete change in how you search — hearing it is only useful if it moves something.

You just heard how hiring really works from the people who do it — university recruiting and headhunting, two different vantage points on the same process. Before it fades, capture what landed.

Reflect · three questions

Write these in your own words

  1. What surprised you? One thing a recruiter said that didn't match how you pictured the process.
  2. What does it change? One concrete adjustment to your search this week — your resume, your bet, your outreach, your timing.
  3. What does it mean for your case? You're making a case for a warm referral this week. What did you hear about how recruiters read candidates that makes your case stronger — or shows you where it's thin?

Recruiters read hundreds of candidates. When one tells you what gets a closer look, that's market data from inside the market — treat it like a signal in your experiment, not just advice.

Capture & submit

Submission type: Text entry. Paste in your answers to the three questions — a few honest sentences each beats a polished page.
