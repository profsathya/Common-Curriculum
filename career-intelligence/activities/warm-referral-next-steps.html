Career Intelligence · Week 6 · Warm Referral

Next Steps for Your Warm Referral Application

You've applied — here's what happens next.

1

We review your application.

2

We send you a link to schedule a 1:1 interview with a member of the CTI team.

3

After the interview, one of two paths: your warm referral email goes out on your behalf — or, if there are concerns to address first, you update your application, and we send the referral once your changes address them.

4

Hear back about the application? Post a message in the Slack channel to let us know — every outcome teaches us how to make the next referral stronger.
