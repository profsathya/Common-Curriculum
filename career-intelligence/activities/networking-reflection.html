Career Intelligence · Week 6 · Own Your Search

Networking Reflection

This activity's goal

Turn tonight's conversations into your network — one real follow-up is worth more than ten connections.

Reflect · three questions

Write these in your own words

  1. Who did you meet? Which conversation stuck with you — and what did they say that you didn't expect?
  2. What changes? One thing you heard tonight that shifts your search — your niche, your targets, or how you talk about yourself.
  3. Who will you follow up with — and what will your message say? Connect on LinkedIn within 24 hours, and open with one specific line from your conversation.

A 10-minute conversation becomes part of your network the moment you follow up — and the specific detail you remembered is what makes them remember you.

Capture & submit

Submission type: Text entry. Paste in your answers to the three questions — a few honest sentences each beats a polished page.
