Career Intelligence · Week 5 · Own Your Search

Warm Referral Application

This activity's goal

Build the case that earns you a warm introduction — the strongest move in a job search — aimed at a role you actually want, with us working alongside you.

This is your application for a warm referral: an introduction to someone at a company where you're genuinely interested in applying, aimed at getting your resume seen for a specific role.

A referral buys a closer look from a person, not just a filter — no guarantees. We refer students whose work we can stand behind, so this step is earned. It's our first round and we're learning as we go — bring your authentic self.

Before you apply

Prerequisite · a soft gate

Access opens through the work you've already done

Show up, do the work, and you're in. Canvas walks you through Week 5 in order — finish any outstanding CI work first.

How to apply — two steps

Step 1 · The written questions

Make your case in three answers

  1. Hand-write your answers first, by yourself — the role, your connection to the company, your evidence (see the questions). Ownership shows in the 1:1.
  2. If you'd like, pressure-test them with the Warm Referral Case Dojo.
  3. Submit them in the Warm Referral: Written Questions survey. (Optional Q4: an artifact that shows your interest — strong.)
Step 2 · Your resume

Upload Resume v3

Upload your latest resume as a PDF in this assignment — aimed at the role you named in the survey, with your edge for it visible.

What happens next

Review → 1:1 → decision

The 10-minute 1:1 is the referral decision

We review your submission, then you book a 10-minute 1:1 for the week of July 13 (link posted once reviews begin). It's scored on three signals:

  • Role clarity — you understand the role and can say why this opportunity.
  • Adequate experience — specific experiences, skills, or habits that show you'd deliver.
  • Purpose alignment — a real connection between you and the company's purpose.

If confirmed, your introduction goes out with your case articulated. Every referral we send is one we fully stand behind — that's what makes yours mean something when it arrives.

The champion test: before you submit, ask — would someone who just read this want to champion you to a colleague?
