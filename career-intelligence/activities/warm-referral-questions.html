Career Intelligence · Week 5 · Own Your Search

Warm Referral — Written Questions

This activity's goal

Your case, in three answers you own — the written half of your warm referral application, and your preparation for the 10-minute 1:1 where the referral decision happens.

Write your answers by hand first, on paper, by yourself — then type them in below. Each question has an AI sparring partner that pressure-tests your answer against the signal your 1:1 will be scored on (role clarity, purpose alignment, adequate experience). It asks questions back; it will not write your answer — the ownership you build here is what shows in the conversation.

The champion test: would someone who just read these answers want to champion you to a colleague?

Submit to Canvas

Submission type: Text entry.

What to submit: when your answers pass the champion test, use the Copy responses into clipboard button at the end of the form, then paste into the Warm Referral: Written Questions assignment on Canvas — and upload your Resume v3 in the Warm Referral Application to complete your application.
