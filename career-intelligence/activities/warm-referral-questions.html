Career Intelligence · Week 5 · Own Your Search

Warm Referral — Written Questions

This activity's goal

Your case, in three answers you own — the written half of your warm referral application, and your preparation for the 10-minute 1:1 where the referral decision happens.

The 1:1 is scored on the same three signals these questions map to: role clarity, purpose alignment, and adequate experience.

Write your answers by hand first, on paper, by yourself. Then — if you'd like — partner with the Warm Referral Case Dojo to pressure-test and sharpen them. Writing them yourself first means you own them; in the 1:1, that ownership shows.

Question 1 · The role · role clarity

Which specific job are you applying for?

Give the company, the role title, and a link to the posting (the warm-path job board is a good starting point).

Then: what does this role actually involve day to day — and why this opportunity, rather than just any job?

Question 2 · The connection · purpose alignment

Why this company, for you?

How do your background, interests, or lived experience connect to this company's purpose and the impact of this role?

The test: write an answer only this company could receive.

Question 3 · Your evidence · adequate experience

What shows you'd deliver?

Name 2–3 specific experiences, skills, or habits that show you'd be dependable, coachable, and able to contribute in this role — each with a concrete example you could walk someone through in conversation.

A claim ("hard worker," "fast learner") becomes evidence the moment a story proves it — attach the story.

Question 4 · Show, don’t tell · optional

An artifact made for this company

Optional, and strong: build one small artifact customized for this company and role — ideally a working product made with Replit or Lovable — that shows you truly understand their business and care about it. Paste the link plus one line on what it shows.

That's your superagency on display: AI raises what you can attempt — the understanding, the judgment, and the care are yours. That's the human value a decision-maker can see, and it turns "please consider me" into "here's the value." Small and real beats grand and vague. If you don't have one, leave it blank — it does not count against you in this round.

The champion test: would someone who just read these answers want to champion you to a colleague?

Capture & submit

Where: type your answers into the numbered boxes in the Week 5 — Warm Referral: Written Questions survey on Canvas — then upload your Resume v3 in the Warm Referral Application assignment to complete your application.
