1 · Hypothesis — what we believe

Stated once, here, in the terms we use. Everything in the other three levels is a way of testing some part of it.

The professional of the AI era answers two questions with evidence — did you tackle a problem you could not have on your own, with the help of AI, and how is what you did better because you were involved? That is superagency plus human value.

Three capacities get them there: self-directed learning, integrative solving, and adaptive building. The courses add these one layer at a time (286 → 349 → 499).

The practice that runs underneath is Symbiotic Thinking — the human-led pursuit of wisdom in partnership with other intelligences, human or artificial — done through two technique pairs, reading/writing and talking/listening, and three habits, slow down, know yourself, take the lead. The six qualities of self-directed action are what we watch move. The reason it holds is self-determination: autonomy, competence and relatedness are designed in, not hoped for.

It runs in two learning environments: the AI Dojo and The Commons.

Each piece earns its place by what it supports. Items marked private live in the cowork repo and are not reachable from this site.

AutomationPush to main → the course Google Docs refresh (scripts/build_course_context.py + Apps Script, how it works). Nothing else runs on its own for the courses yet; submission checks are by hand. Supports: level 3 (the Dojo reads the doc).
Design logsPer course, the status of each sub-experiment and a dated log of what changed: 286 · 349 · 499. Supports: level 2 — the source of the cells above.
The inventorycourse-system-inventory-2026-09-02.mdprivate — every store and process, who writes and reads it, and the nine seams. Supports: this page; consult it before adding anything here.
The rule for this level: a new tool, page or document gets a row here only after it says which of the other three levels it supports. If it cannot, it is not machinery, it is clutter.
