Three courses, one framework

CST 286 · CST 349 · CST 499 · Fall 2026
The three fall courses share one way of working, and each adds a layer to it. This page puts them side by side.
How the three fall courses compare, and what they share
Aspect of the course CST 286a physics topic as the focus CST 349professional development CST 499capstone
What you learn to do Take charge of learning something you care about: choose it, get clear on why it matters to you, decide what having learned it would look like, gather the evidence that you have, and carry what you learned into a new setting. Direct your own growth as a professional, and combine two or more disciplines — or the viewpoints of the people your work serves — to find a new space of value you can offer. Direct your own learning, integrate perspectives, and build: design a real piece of work, improve it through rounds of iteration, and finish with something that strengthens your resume.
Where you practice it A physics topic you choose because it interests you. Your own professional development — the subject of this course is you. Your capstone project: a portfolio piece you can point people to.
The layers Directing your own learning Directing your own learning + integrating perspectivesProfessionals describe this as going from T‑shaped toward comb‑shaped: deep in one area at first, and every discipline you add becomes another tooth. Directing your own learning + integrating perspectives + building
How every course starts Shared across all threeIn conversation — with your teacher, with your peers, with AI, and in combinations of all three. Out of that practice grows Symbiotic Thinking: the human-led practice of pursuing wisdom in partnership with other intelligences, human or artificial.
The two questions Shared across all threeEarly in the semester, once the conversations are running and you are into the work, two questions arrive and stay for the rest of the course: Did you tackle a problem you couldn’t have on your own, with the help of AI? And how is what you did better because you were involved? You may still be working out your answers when the course ends — these questions are meant to be lived with. Your value as a professional rests on being able to answer both, with evidence.
The six qualities Shared across all threeUnderneath the coursework run six qualities of self-directed action, woven through the whole semester. Each activity connects to a few of them by name, and you and the course watch how they move as you work.
