The form

Task 1 · My claim and three cards

What does a visitor see on the first screen of your final poster? Include:
  • your claim, and the line under it
  • the title of each of your three cards (the work, the decision, and the obstacle or open question), with the one line that sits under each title
  • one sentence on where your work stands
(Claim only what a visitor can see proven when they open your cards.)

Task 2 · The milestones from my first version

Which of the milestones you set in your first version are done, and which are not? (For each one that is not done, write what happened and what a visitor will see instead.)

Task 3 · My final poster

Where can a reviewer open your final poster? (Share a link that opens without asking for access. If your tool cannot share a link, add screenshots of the first screen and of each card to your submission.)
