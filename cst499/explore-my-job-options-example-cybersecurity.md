# Example conversation: cybersecurity in aerospace and manufacturing

CST499 job search group · Explore my job options · Example from Friday 6 November

This is a copy of the second example conversation from Friday, for anyone who can’t open the CSUMB ChatGPT link. A student pasted the prompt from the [Explore my job options page](https://profsathya.github.io/Common-Curriculum/cst499/explore-my-job-options.html) into ChatGPT and answered its questions. Read it to see how the coach asks about the role and the area you have in mind, and then searches for live postings that match.

**Student:**

*(The student pasted the full Explore my job options prompt.)*

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

I’ll be your Sensei and guide us one step at a time. The Challenger will ask for specifics when needed, and the Auditor will check the job postings. You make the decisions.

What kind of role do you have in mind?

**Student:**

Cybersecurity

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

What other area do you care about or know something about—such as healthcare, education, agriculture, finance, government, or entertainment?

**Student:**

Are there other options?

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

Yes. Cybersecurity also connects to transportation, energy and water systems, aerospace, manufacturing, retail, sports, nonprofits, and media. You do not need to be an expert; an interest or experience outside class can give us a starting point.

Which area interests you most, and why?

**Student:**

aerospace and manufacturing

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

What draws you to aerospace and manufacturing?

**Student:**

I just find it interesting

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

The Challenger is stepping in to make that interest more specific. What is one thing in aerospace or manufacturing that catches your attention—for example, spacecraft, aircraft, robots, or how things get made?

**Student:**

We can revisit this later, I am curious see what type of jobs are available

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

We can leave that open and explore cybersecurity jobs in aerospace and manufacturing.

Is there anything you want to add before I search, such as a class project, work experience, or a preferred location in California?

**Student:**

Not right now

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**
