# Integrative Solving Reflection — future revision note

Recorded 22 November 2026 from Sathya's grading discussion. This is a note for a future offering; the published Fall 2026 assignment is unchanged.

The assignment should state its learning purpose directly: students are evaluating whether a broader perspective would improve their project's ability to meet its goal. They should not add a second domain merely to fit the T-shaped-skills frame. A supported conclusion that broadening would not help the project now should be valid.

Revise the purpose statement, Route B questions, self-check, worked examples, and AI Dojo prompt together. Route B should let a student explain either (a) a project-specific gap that another domain could help fill, with a first step, or (b) why no additional domain would improve the current project, with a project-specific reason and a way to test or revisit that judgment. Keep the two routes comparable in points. Make clear that naming a domain alone is not the goal; the connection to project quality is.

Before reusing the assignment, review the student responses and grading discussion from Fall 2026 for examples of where the present wording encouraged a forced domain or confused the purpose.
