Week 16 · Dec 14 – Dec 20
Beyond the grade
What you leave with
  1. The work. Anyone should be able to look at it and immediately recognize your value in it.
  2. What made the work possible. Knowing what you are actually good at, choosing your own direction, planning and executing to create impact.

Your ability to explain both of these things is how people will be able to understand what you bring to the table.

What you build here is meant to be used.

At the end of the course you can earn up to five referrals from Prof. Sathya’s network. Each one is your choice of two kinds: a referral toward a job posting you are applying to, or an introduction to a mentor in the field. An application with a referral leads to an interview about 40% of the time; the industry average across all applications is about 3%.

Referrals go to students whose week-13 work clears the published bar. A job referral needs a short written case for that job — this role, this work, why you fit. Mentor introductions open once the bar is cleared. Building social capital by connecting with professionals is key to your success, and this is our attempt at loaning you some of ours.

When we meet. Full class meets on the kickoff Wednesdays — weeks 1, 2, 6, 10, 14, and 16. Friday sessions and the remaining Wednesdays are for interviews, booked through the scheduling link on Canvas.
