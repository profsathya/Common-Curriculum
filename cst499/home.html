The big picture All six sprints on one screen, and what you get out of this beyond the grade

Six sprints across sixteen instructional weeks. Hover any sprint name to see what it is, why it comes where it does, and what you have at the end of it. Two sprints overlap on purpose: your niche and your gap sharpen each other through weeks 3–5, and your job search starts in week 13, before your build is finished. Fall Break is the week of 23 November.

Weeks 10–13 · Oct 26 – Nov 22
Sprint 4
Sprint 4 · Own your searchWhat this sprint is. Three weeks running your own job search: choosing your targets, rebuilding your resume on what you have proved, and testing an approach instead of waiting.Why it comes here. Own is the word that carries this sprint. No career center, instructor, or job board is going to run your search for you. A search you own is one you can read and adjust when it is not working, and it is the part of hiring you actually control.What you have at the end. A resume and portfolio built on this semester’s evidence, a search you have already tested once, and interview practice on your own work.
Run your job search yourself, as something you test and adjust
Weeks 13–15 · Nov 16 – Dec 13
Sprint 5
Sprint 5 · Wrap up and defendWhat this sprint is. One week to finish your poster and presentation, review a classmate’s work, defend your own, and present at the Capstone Festival.Why it comes here. Every hiring process ends in a version of the same question — what did you do, and how do you know it worked? This is where you practice answering it in front of people who ask it professionally.What you have at the end. A portfolio you could send tomorrow, a written plan for what comes next, and a public presentation behind you.
Present it, explain it, and leave with it

Beyond the grade
What you build here is meant to be used.

In Sprint 4, Prof. Sathya can send a referral email on your behalf — an introduction to someone he knows at a company, backed by his name. It is optional, and it goes to students whose week-13 artifact clears the published bar, which is why it comes after you have built something.

We are also planning an invitation-only virtual networking session with engineers from industry, so you finish the semester with people in your corner as well as a portfolio.
