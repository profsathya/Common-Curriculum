What Would I Do Anyway

Due Friday 4 September, 11:59 p.m. · about 30 minutes

Where this fits. This week connects what the market is asking for, work that holds your attention, and the evidence you want to build next — and next week you turn all three into a short professional introduction (the full picture is on Read the Market). Today is the middle one.

Today you will have
  • Six specific occasions when work held your attention.
  • A pattern shared by at least three of them, tested against a second reading.
  • Where that pattern sits next to what the market is asking for.

What you are building on

Your market read from Wednesday. You need it for box 4 only — leave it closed for the first three, so your answers are not shaped by what would be convenient. Nothing else is assumed.

Why this sits in a market week

The market has shifted toward work that takes sustained attention. The first pass at most technical tasks is quick now; what carries value is the part that comes after — the problem someone keeps working on, the detail they follow further than they were asked to. Knowing which kinds of work do that for you is what lets you aim at the openings worth aiming at, and it is a large part of what makes a professional introduction sound like a person rather than a profile.

So this is not a detour from the market work. Wednesday told you where the openings are. Today tells you which of them are worth your effort.

Read all six moments again, slowly. Then do one of three things to what you wrote in box 2: revise it, confirm it with the evidence that supports it, or qualify it — say where it holds and where it does not. Confirming is a real outcome; a second reading does not have to disagree with the first.

Your own AI DojoA conversation, if the boxes are not getting you there.

I am working out what kinds of work consistently hold my attention. Interview me about it — one question at a time, and wait for my answer before you ask the next one.

Start from specific occasions rather than general descriptions: ask me about times I kept going or lost track of the hour, what I was doing, who it was for, and what kept me engaged. Do not summarise me until I have given you at least six occasions. Then play back the pattern you heard, and I will tell you where it is off.
