The weekly rhythm, the four kinds of work, and how your grade is calculated.

2. Weekly Rhythm

  • Wednesday class session: Introduce the week's core concept, run paired critiques or in-class activities, and align on what good work looks like.
  • Wednesday / Friday Own Your Progress (OYP) prep: Short preparation steps due Wednesday or Friday at 11:59 pm that build the pieces you need for the next Graded Item.
  • Friday class session: Working check-ins, troubleshooting blockers, and Sprint Interviews at the end of each sprint.
  • Monday Graded Item: Major deliverables are due Monday at 11:59 pm following the week so you have the weekend to finish after Friday feedback.
