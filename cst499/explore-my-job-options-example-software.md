# Example conversation: software engineering in healthcare and government

CST499 job search group · Explore my job options · Example from Friday 6 November

This is a copy of the first example conversation from Friday, for anyone who can’t open the CSUMB ChatGPT link. A student pasted the prompt from the [Explore my job options page](https://profsathya.github.io/Common-Curriculum/cst499/explore-my-job-options.html) into ChatGPT and answered its questions. Read it to see how the coach asks about the role and the area you have in mind, and then searches for live postings that match.

**Student:**

*(The student pasted the full Explore my job options prompt.)*

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

I’ll be your Sensei for this session. I’ll bring in the Challenger when we need more specifics and the Auditor when we check job postings. You make the choices.

What kind of role do you have in mind?

**Student:**

Software engineer

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

What other area do you care about or know something about, such as healthcare, education, agriculture, finance, government, or entertainment?

**Student:**

Healthcare and government

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

What would you like to add before I search? A brief description of your capstone or another project would help me connect your work to software roles in healthcare and government.

**Student:**

Not right now

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

I found a small shortlist: one general software role, two healthcare/government roles, and one surprise. Your capstone skills and graduation date are still unconfirmed, so these are possibilities to assess together.

### 1. Roles your capstone may prepare you for

Your CS degree and software engineering goal make this a plausible path; I would need your project details to judge your preparation.

| Software Engineer — New Grad, 2027 Start | Databricks | Mountain View or San Francisco | Builds software for a data and AI platform. Seeks programming, algorithms, and project experience; graduation must be fall 2026 or spring 2027. | Posting |

The employer page displayed an application button when checked.

### 2. Roles your other areas open

Your interests in healthcare and government give you two concrete directions for applying your CS education.

| Job title | Employer | Location | Why it fits me | Link to the posting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Associate Software Engineer — College Grad 2027 | Solace | Redwood City; three office days weekly | Builds databases, APIs, and interfaces supporting patient advocacy. Seeks December 2026–June 2027 graduates; starts July 2027. | Posting |

The Solace details came from its indexed employer posting; its application page requires JavaScript, so I could not independently confirm its live application status. The state posting was readable.

### 3. One surprise

Your combined healthcare and government interests also point toward helping public hospitals select and manage technology.

| Job title | Employer | Location | Why it fits me | Link to the posting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IT Procurement Analyst — Information Technology Associate | California Department of State Hospitals | Sacramento or facilities in five other California counties | Uses technical understanding for technology purchasing, business analysis, and planning. Primarily technology management rather than coding. Deadline: November 12. | Posting |

Despite “hybrid” in the title, the posting specifies at least four office days weekly.

Auditor’s note: State IT Associate qualifications include an education route, but hiring eligibility also matters. IT exams are currently offline until an anticipated November 17 return, which needs checking before applying.

Please open at least two posting links to check that they still accept applications.

Which role surprised you most, and why?

**Student:**

Are these new grad positions?

Can we work through step by step to put together a competitive application to these postings?

**ChatGPT · job search coach:**

Databricks and Solace explicitly advertise new-grad roles. The two state jobs are not specifically new-grad postings; they have an education-based eligibility route. I should have made that distinction clearer. Meeting minimum qualifications does not establish how competitive you are.

Yes—we can work through one application at a time: check eligibility, match your projects to the requirements, tailor your résumé, and prepare any required statements.

Which posting would you like to start with, and why does it appeal to you?

In the original conversation, each “Posting” was a link to the job posting. Those links are not copied here, and postings close over time. To find one, search the job title and the employer’s name.
