CST499 · Course Premise

Why This Course Works This Way

Entry-level software hiring has shifted: AI can generate boilerplate in minutes, so employers hire for what you can scope, verify, and explain under questioning.

1. What Changed in the Job Market

  • A class project on a resume is no longer enough by itself: Employers know AI tools can generate a working CRUD app in an afternoon.
  • What stands out now is verifiable engineering judgment: Picking a real problem with an actual user, testing where AI output fails, holding your work to a professional bar, and explaining your trade-offs clearly in person.
  • Every assignment in CST499 builds that proof: Instead of disconnected homework, each week adds one layer to the same capstone artifact and career case.

2. The Six Qualities Every Sprint Practices

1. Self-knowledgeKnow what holds your attention, what you can already prove, and where your gaps are.
2. Working with uncertaintyScope messy problems, test assumptions against real evidence, and adjust when a path breaks.
3. Self-regulationSet a weekly rhythm, track your own progress, and meet milestones without waiting to be chased.
4. AdaptabilityPick up new tools and adjacent domains quickly when your project needs them.
5. InitiativeFind real users, maintainers, or mentors and put your work in front of them early.
6. Owning the outcomeDefine the quality bar for your work and verify that the finished software meets it.

3. Why Interviews Are 50% of the Grade

You can use AI throughout the course to brainstorm, draft, debug, and get feedback. The Sprint Interviews (50%) and in-class mid-term check that the understanding, decisions, and code ownership are genuinely yours - the exact same test you face in a technical job interview.
