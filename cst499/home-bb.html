Week 3 - Audit your evidence, name your gapSep 7
Week 3 walkthrough →
Wed Sep 9: Work session - Goal: develop a solid Project Proposal (Prof. Paige in room 110 12-1pm)  |  Fri Sep 11: Work session - Goal: develop a solid Project Proposal (Prof. Paige on Zoom, Prof. Sathya at bit.ly/profsathya)
OYP Goal: Inventory the evidence I already have to show a hiring manager Recommended: Fri 11 Sep · 100 pts (0% weight)
  • List every piece of work you could put in front of an employer today - projects, class assignments, things you built on your own, contributions, anything. Do not filter yet.
  • Next to each one, write what it proves you can do and who or what could verify it - a repo, a live link, a person.
  • Mark the ones you could walk someone through in detail right now. That shorter list is your real evidence.
OYP Goal: Name the gap my capstone needs to close Recommended: Mon 14 Sep · 100 pts (0% weight)
  • Take the niche you are aiming at - the kind of work, in the kind of company, solving the kind of problem.
  • Set your evidence list against what those jobs ask for and mark what is missing.
  • Write the gap as one sentence you could say out loud. That sentence is what your Project Proposal will answer.
Week 4 - Propose your projectSep 14
Wed Sep 16: Work session - Goal: develop a solid Project Proposal (Prof. Paige in room 110 12-1pm)  |  Fri Sep 18: Work session - Goal: develop a solid Project Proposal (Prof. Paige on Zoom, Prof. Sathya at bit.ly/profsathya)
OYP Goal: Know what makes my proposal credible before I write it Recommended: Fri 18 Sep · 100 pts (0% weight)
  • Read the Project Proposal criteria and restate in your own words what the proposal has to convince a reader of.
  • Find two real examples of work in your problem space - a merged contribution, a shipped feature, an issue someone resolved - and note what made them credible.
  • Draft the one sentence a reviewer will judge: what you will deliver and how it closes your gap.
Graded Project Proposal Due Mon 21 Sep · 100 pts

Goal: Learn how to scope a credible project that solves a real need, goes beyond what AI builds out of the box, and defines how your work will be judged.
