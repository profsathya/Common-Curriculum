Sprint 4

Own the search while finishing the proof

Weeks 13–15 · Nov 16 – Dec 13
Three weeks to turn the new evidence into a market case and run your search as an experiment, while the last of the build finishes.
What you will be able to do
  • Turn “I am applying to jobs” into a stated bet, with a fair test and a signal you would accept
  • Rebuild a resume around evidence rather than coursework
  • Make a written case for yourself strong enough that someone would put their name behind it
  • Hold up in an interview about work you actually did

Optional
The warm referral.

If your week-13 artifact set clears the published bar, you can ask Prof. Sathya for an introduction to someone he knows at a company. You write the case; he decides whether he can stand behind it. It is optional, it is not part of your grade, and it is the reason the artifact-quality check sits where it does.
