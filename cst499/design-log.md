## The shared start

- **PARTIAL — Conversation as the opening move (teacher · peers · AI, in combinations).**
  Evidence: conversations are the assessment spine, designed and on the site — 3–5 per student, group-first (Sprint 0 group interviews by week 2 → proposal week 4 → group rounds weeks 4–5 → triage → 1-on-1 from week 6 → final defense weeks 15–16); Canvas weeks 1–2 carry them.
  Missing: those are assessment conversations. The framework's opening move — the course *starting* in conversation with teacher, peers, and AI, growing into Symbiotic Thinking — is absent; Part 0 currently opens with the job-market data.

- **NOT YET — Symbiotic Thinking, named and practiced.**
  Evidence: nearest neighbors exist — the AI-transparency paragraph on `why-this-course.html` and "how to keep the thinking yours" on home.
  Missing: the named practice appears nowhere.

## The self-directed learning layer

- **PARTIAL — Choose your own gap and pursue it.**
  Evidence: designed and built — start with the student; the niche and the gap named in Part 1; hybrid paths (own project / faculty research / industry / certification) inside one structure; problem-space framing on the site.
  Missing: two open design questions — how idea-less students get matched (Paige's sharpest worry), and how mentored-project students reconcile a mentor-set project with a self-named gap.

- **DONE — Define what success looks like, and demonstrate it.**
  Evidence: grading locked and student-facing on `understand-the-course-design.html` — 50/50 mirror mechanic, completion alone is an F and defending a little earns the D, conversation standard = explain it to a human without jargon, no mid-course retakes with one cumulative end retake capped at C, late 10%.
  At the review gate rather than missing: per-assignment points and due dates (Canvas weeks 1–2, built and unpublished), the certificate bar.

## The integrating-perspectives layer

- **PARTIAL — Combine disciplines and stakeholder viewpoints to find value.**
  Evidence: the problem spaces already cross disciplines (kinesiology, aircraft inspection, machine learning), so students practice integration by construction.
  Missing: nothing *teaches* or names the skill — combining perspectives to find a new space of value appears on no page.

## The building layer — the layer 499 adds

- **DONE — Build and iterate portfolio pieces that strengthen the resume.**
  Evidence: two build sprints producing two finished pieces sit at the center of the course (weeks 6–11), deliberately before the search; the full sprint arc is built and on the site.
  Notes: the two-piece count was our proposal and was never explicitly settled with Paige; the problem-space directory still owes 5–8 more verified projects (security, networking, data science, games).

- **NOT YET — Every activity aligned to a named subset of the six qualities.**
  Evidence: principle decided 10–11 Aug — the qualities are woven throughout the course rather than treated as a start/end measure; each activity carries a subset chosen by its focus; Map Your Curiosity is out as a course activity; the opening Know-Yourself activity carries self-knowledge and probably self-regulation.
  Missing: the activity-by-activity mapping for this course.

- **NOT YET — Movement on those qualities watched per activity.**
  Evidence: decided 11 Aug — conversations with AI Sensei run throughout the course (the two-conversation week 1–2 / week 6–8 design is superseded; note on the 8/10 plan file).
  Missing: how each activity's conversations or artifacts record movement, and which parts of the plan file survive per conversation.
