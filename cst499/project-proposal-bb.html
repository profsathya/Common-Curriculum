Project Proposal

Due Monday 21 Sep, 11:59 pm · 100 points · Graded items (40% of course grade)
Builds on
Learn what your target market asks for and which gap to close (Read the Market & Find Your Gap).
This goal
Learn how to scope a credible project that solves a real need, goes beyond what AI builds out of the box, and defines how your work will be judged.
Feeds into
Learn what new skills your build requires (Learning Plan) and test your reasoning (Mid-Term).

    Identify one or more roles you are aiming for. For each, link two or three postings you are using as the bar.

    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  2. 2
    State the gap in your preparation briefly: what those postings ask for that you cannot show yet.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  3. 3
    Explain how you got from the gap to this project.
    1. What you considered - problem spaces, partner projects, ideas of your own.
    2. What you ruled out, and why.
    3. What makes the one you chose the right fit for the gap.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  4. 4
    Describe the project.
    1. What you will build or make.
    2. Who it is for - a named person, team or kind of user.
    3. The evidence that this is a real need for that person, team or user: what you observed, who you asked and what they said, or the data you found - each with its source.
    4. What they will do with it that they cannot do now.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  5. 5
    Test what AI can build of this today.
    1. What you tried, and with what (ai.studio or another tool).
    2. What came out.
    3. What it could not do, or got wrong.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  6. 6
    Identify what you will bring that the tool does not - judgment, domain knowledge, testing with real users, the part that needs a person. This is a goal you aim at and can adjust; the mid-term and the final report ask what you brought.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  7. 7
    Set the scope.
    1. What will exist by the mid-term (week 8).
    2. What will exist by week 12.
    3. What is out.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  8. 8
    Name the evidence.
    1. What a hiring manager will be able to see at the end - repo, write-up, demo, data.
    2. What external verifications you are considering to demonstrate quality, impact and your human value - a user who tried it, someone in the field who reviewed it, data from real use.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
  9. 9
    Write your rubric: three to five categories, each with A, B and C descriptors, detailed enough for someone else to grade you with. Team project: say what you are accountable for.
    Check your answer:
    Quotes your words, names what is missing from the checklist above, and asks one question.
