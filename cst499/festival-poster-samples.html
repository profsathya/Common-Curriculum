Sample Student, CST499 software track, contributing to Kolibri

The gap I chose

Postings for new-grad roles ask for code shipped through review, and mine had only been reviewed by my own team. I chose Kolibri, an offline learning platform for schools with little internet, where a bug that fails quietly hurts the most.

9 of 12postings asked for code review

  1. Contribute to a real project instead of building my own app

    What I was facing
    Build my own app, join a partner project, or fix bugs in open source.
    How I knew
    The postings asked for outside review. My own app would only be reviewed by me.
    What I chose and gave up
    Open source. I gave up owning something from start to finish.
    What it says about me
    I choose the evidence I need over what looks impressive.

  2. Get a clean baseline before writing any fix The obstacle

    What I was facing
    11 tests failed on my laptop before I had changed anything.
    How I knew
    I could not tell my mistakes apart from my setup's.
    What I chose and gave up
    I moved to Linux through WSL and gave up a week.
    What it says about me
    I want to prove my change did what I say it did.
    Windows
    WSL
    The same test run: 11 failures on Windows, 0 under WSL

  3. Ask the maintainers before widening the fix

    What I was facing
    The bug I was fixing also existed in two other places.
    How I knew
    I reproduced all three and wrote down the steps.
    What I chose and gave up
    I asked first, and they wanted one small pull request per place. I gave up a bigger change.
    What it says about me
    The people who own the code decide what done means.

    "One PR per import path, please. That is easier for us to review."

    A maintainer's reply on the issue thread

How I directed AI

  1. Claude Code gave meA fix for the one case in the bug report.
    I redirected it"Find every place with the same pattern." It found two more.
  2. Claude Code gave meA test that used a fake file copy.
    I redirected it"Test against a real copy that fails halfway."
  3. Claude Code gave meA three-paragraph pull request description.
    I redirected it"Match the recently merged ones: short, one change." It became five lines.

The AI did most of the typing. I decided what the work needed, and I ran everything myself before trusting it.

Where it stands

My first pull request is open, and I have answered one round of review. It is not merged yet.

What's next

Get both pull requests merged, then keep contributing until the maintainers know my name.

When I don't understand something, I set up a situation where I can watch it happen.

Sample Student, CST499 certificate track, CompTIA Security+

The gap I chose

Entry-level analyst postings ask for a baseline certificate and hands-on experience with security tools. I knew the concepts from class, but I had never investigated anything myself.

10 of 12postings named Security+

  1. Security+ instead of another certificate

    What I was facing
    Security+, AWS Cloud Practitioner, or a cyber-defense certificate.
    How I knew
    I counted which ones the postings named.
    What I chose and gave up
    Security+. I gave up cloud, which some postings also wanted.
    What it says about me
    I let the evidence make the choice, not my guess.
    Security+
    10 AWS
    3
    Postings that named each certificate, out of 12

  2. Add a home lab instead of studying for the exam alone

    What I was facing
    A certificate alone looks like everyone else's.
    How I knew
    Working analysts document their investigations step by step.
    What I chose and gave up
    A free virtual lab instead of used equipment or online labs only. I gave up some study time.
    What it says about me
    I want something I can show, not only a score.
    KaliUbuntuWindowsIsolated network
    My lab: three virtual machines that cannot reach the internet

  3. Pause Security+ to learn networking first The obstacle

    What I was facing
    My practice scores stayed between 60% and 65% for three weeks.
    How I knew
    I sorted my missed questions. Most were look-alike terms that need networking.
    What I chose and gave up
    Two weeks on ports, protocols and subnets. I gave up two weeks on my exam date.
    What it says about me
    When I am stuck, I look for the reason in my own mistakes.
    Practice exam scores by week

How I directed AI

  1. ChatGPT gave meA study plan that would fit anyone.
    I redirected it"Here are the 40 questions I missed. What do they have in common?" I checked its groups against the exam objectives.
  2. ChatGPT gave meTextbook definitions of the terms.
    I redirected it"Quiz me only on the pairs I confuse, and use my lab as the example."
  3. ChatGPT gave meAn exercise that scanned a public website.
    I redirected it"Keep everything inside my isolated lab." We built an exercise I could run safely.

The AI made my practice faster. I decided what to practice, and I checked it against the exam objectives and my own results.

Where it stands

The exam is on December 12. Two lab write-ups are done, and my latest practice score is 81%.

What's next

Pass the exam, add one lab investigation a month, and aim for an analyst internship.
