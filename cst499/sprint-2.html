Week 6 — Onboard · Sep 28
Write your onboarding noteKey item
One to two pages: what this project or certification actually involves, who it serves, how contributions get accepted, and what this community treats as finished. Write it so someone who has never heard of the project could follow it.
Week 7 — Reproduce it before you solve it · Oct 5
Reproduce the problem, or document itCheckpoint
Show the problem happening on your own machine, with the steps you took to get there. On a certification path, finish the first module and submit your notes instead.
Week 8 — Plan the build · Oct 12
Write your evidence and build planCheckpoint
What you will build over the next two weeks, and what specifically will count as proof that it worked. Name the tests, the reviewers, or the users.
Class time is yours to build in from here. Optional deep-work sessions run alongside — come if it helps you get it done.
Week 9 — Ship the first milestone · Oct 19
Ship your first working milestone, with an evidence logKey item
Something that runs, plus a log of what you tried, what broke, and what you changed. A maintainer, a user, or a mentor should be able to look at it without you in the room.
Come to your Sprint 2 conversationIn person
We pick one entry from your evidence log and go deep on it: what you did, why you did it that way, what broke, and what you changed. You will not know which entry in advance.
