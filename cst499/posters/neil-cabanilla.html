Decision · Make progress legible

Design decision in the current modal

I put earned and locked achievements in one place. The interface gives players a way to distinguish accomplishments from goals they have not reached. The visible earned/locked distinction in the existing modal is the artifact behind this decision.

Earned · completedLocked · still ahead

Labels above summarize the distinction; they are not a screenshot of the application. I will add a real modal screenshot and record what a classmate understood without prompting.

Open evidence

Why this choice matters

Showing both states can help players tell where they stand. But the locked goals will only be useful if they correspond to meaningful DisceRNA gameplay milestones. I need to agree on those milestones with the gameplay teammate, then test whether the labels and layout are clear.

Tradeoff

Showing more information in the modal could feel crowded, so I will watch where a classmate hesitates and revise the wording or layout based on that feedback.
