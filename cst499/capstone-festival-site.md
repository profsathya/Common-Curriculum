## Home page
- Header: CSUMB logo · School of Computing and Design · B.S. Computer Science
- Banner: "Sample site. These students and projects are made up."
- Eyebrow: CST499 Capstone
- Title: Capstone Festival
- Date line: December 2026 · date and rooms to be confirmed
- Intro: Find a student by name, project or kind of work. Then go to their table and ask them about it.
- Search placeholder: Name, project or category (searches name, project, category and room)
- Category chips: All · Open source and software · Security and certificates · Data and AI · Web and mobile apps · Games and interactive
- Rooms list (tap a room to filter): Room 101 software · Room 104 security · Room 110 data · Room 112 web · Room 118 games

## Student page
- Photo (only if the student opted in), name, category
- Room and Table in large type
- Project: title + short description
- Claim: "Claim coming soon. [First name] will add the one sentence their Interactive Poster sets out to show."
- At the table: "Ask [first name] to walk you through one decision they made and what it cost them."
- Demo box "Student's choice: photo": checkbox "Publish my photo on the festival site".
  Checked = photo space shown; unchecked = no photo space. Every photo gets the same professional filter.
  "Try it with a photo" previews a photo on the device only; nothing is uploaded.
