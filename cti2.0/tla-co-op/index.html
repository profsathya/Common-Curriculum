      I think it's a good idea, with one change. Put the video full width with the strip of sections under it, but make each card in the strip short: just the number, title and length.       What gets better:

      • The video gets about 40% wider on a laptop, and for a recording of faces in a gallery that matters more than anything else on the page.
      • The strip can slide along by itself as one clip ends and the next begins, so people see where they are in the session.
      • On a phone, swiping through the sections feels natural.

      What it costs:

      • Nobody sees the whole session at a glance anymore. A carousel shows maybe four sections at a time, and the current side list works as an agenda. For a colleague deciding whether to watch at all, seeing all ten titles at once helps.
      • That's why I'd keep the cards to titles only. Long descriptions inside carousel cards make the cards tall and uneven, and then the strip looks cluttered.

      To soften the first cost, the left and right arrows can move two sections at a time, and a small "1 of 10" counter shows where you are.
