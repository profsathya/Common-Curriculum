The CTI Landscape

The 2000-foot view on one page: the foundational questions we are asking, the pilots asking them in specific forms, and how what we learn flows back up. Refreshed monthly. For the how-to of daily work, see the Team Guidebook.

What we are doing: testing the CTI 2.0 framework — in its entirety or one component at a time — through small 4–8 week pilots, and collating what we learn into a comprehensive picture that informs how human development adapts in the age of AI.

The foundational questions

Three questions sit above everything. Every pilot below asks a specific, testable form of F1 — and some also test F2. What a pilot learns flows back up as evidence on these.

F1 · Framework Do the CTI 2.0 capabilities — Self-Directed Learner → Integrative Solver → Adaptive Builder, integrated by Symbiotic Thinking — describe what AI-age work actually requires? And does developing them produce human-valuable performance in real work?
F2 · Diffusion Can colleges, community partners, and industry adopt the framework in forms that keep it working — spreading with the substance intact, not as fashion?
F3 · North Star Does the middle of society participate and thrive in the AI age? Downstream of F1 and F2, on a longer clock than any pilot. It justifies the work; no single pilot is measured by it.

How the learning flows back up

Each pilot runs in short loops and captures what it learns in the team repo (conversations → decisions → the pilot's status file). Those findings flow up the layers: pilot evidence accumulates on F1 and F2, cross-pilot patterns get promoted into the framework and methodology, and the foundational questions themselves get sharpened — or falsified. F3 stays overhead as the reason for all of it. This page is the picture those promoted learnings assemble.
