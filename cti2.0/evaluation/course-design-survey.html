New comments ask for efficient use of AI agents while keeping human judgment, and for practice using AI as a tutor without becoming dependent on it to do the work. One respondent wants a clearer explanation of 'T-shaped' skills. Earlier comments ask for more human conversation, earlier project guidance, and a clearer link between graded work and project progress.

Positive comments

Entry-level hiring
“I thought the video was interesting and gave me some insight into what has been happening in hiring at the entry level that I didn't have before.”

Improvement comments

Assignments
“I hate these assignment with a vitriol unmatched. They do nothing but annoy me and don't test me in a way that is actually helpful. It is a distraction from my actual project”
Human conversation
“Typing to an AI doesn't help me practice communication, and the endless follow-up questions that it asked were more frustrating than helpful.”
AI agency
“how to know when to push back on the AI and how to use it to improve your own skills as a tutor rather than as the main work producer.”
T-shaped skills
“is T-shaped supposed to be multiple focusses, or multiple strengths in one focus?”
