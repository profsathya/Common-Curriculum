A framework that helps all learners build Superagency and Human Value in the age of AI.

computingtalentinitiative.org

The Question We Work On

As AI grows, how do you identify and nurture the human qualities that matter most, and how do you know they are growing?

The difficulty is one of pace. The technology moves quickly and crosses every boundary at once, and the systems that prepare people for work move deliberately and within their own. Holding both is the real work: moving at the speed the technology sets while staying grounded in academic values.

Nobody has the full answer yet. We are working it out with real learners, in the open.

Our Approach

  1. We frame the problem.We ask what AI changes across human knowledge and work, and which qualities hold their value in every discipline.
  2. We approach the work.We learn alongside students, on ground that is new for faculty too. The work runs as small experiments of six to ten weeks, each a rigorous build-measure-learn loop that identifies, nurtures, and tracks the qualities behind self-directed action.
  3. We understand our audience.Students who begin at community colleges and regional public universities. Fifteen years of knowing where they start and where they need to arrive.

Our Ask

React to the framework. Question the evidence. Partner with us on the next round of pilots, on your campus or in your region.
