What We Are Building

CTI is an R&D institute at the intersection of AI, learning science, and workforce, serving California's large middle. We work on a fundamental question: as AI grows, how do you identify and nurture the human qualities that matter most, agency, curiosity, judgment, adaptability, and the ability to work with uncertainty?

A framework, as a hypothesis. Grounded in research, inspired by what AI makes possible, and testable, resting on Symbiotic Thinking and three durable capabilities (Self-Directed Learner, Integrative Solver, Adaptive Builder).
Short experiments that iterate. Learning experiences that average six weeks; we measure, learn, and improve. We grow these capabilities in our own work as we ask learners to grow theirs, so we co-evolve.
