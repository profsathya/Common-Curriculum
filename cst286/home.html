Get Truly Good at Something You Care About

You choose the pursuit. Physics shows you how it really works underneath. The depth makes you better at it — and the habit of going deep stays with you.
This semester you take something you already care about — a sport, a game, an instrument, a craft, a hobby — and get genuinely better at it by understanding how it really works. Physics is the lens this course hands you for that The deep diveWhat it means. Getting underneath your pursuit to the level where you can explain how it really works — with physics as the lens this course hands you.Why it matters. Understanding the mechanism is what turns “I do this” into “I can make this better on purpose.”What a good one looks like. You can show how it works, show you got better at the pursuit because of the depth, and run the same process on something new.: the mechanics under a jump shot, the acoustics under a guitar, the thermodynamics under baking. In the second half of the term you choose a second interest and run the same process again, more on your own — because the skill this course builds is the habit of going deep, and by December you will have seen it work twice.
Two things come out of this semester:
  1. Your pursuit, genuinely improved. You will be better at the thing itself, and able to show the before and after.
  2. A process for going deep that you own. A repeatable way to get underneath any interest or goal and come back better at it.
Explaining both of these out loud is how the depth is verified. That is why you sit down and walk your instructor through your own work each sprint, and why those interviews are half your grade.
