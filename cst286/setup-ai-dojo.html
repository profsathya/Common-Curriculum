Set Up Your CST286 AI Dojo

Once, before the end of week 1 · about 10 minutes

Purpose

By the end of week 1 you should be able to say how CST286 works — how the semester is put together, what it asks of you, how your grade is worked out, and where you go when you need to find something.

What you will be able to explain

Four things about CST286, in your own words:

  • How the course is structured — the shape of the semester, and what a normal week looks like.
  • What is expected of you — what you are asked to do, and by when.
  • How grading works — what earns points, and how the pieces add up to a grade.
  • Where to find things — where you go for the schedule, for an assignment, or for an answer at 11 p.m.

Two ways to get there

Use one, or both. They end with you writing those four in your own words, they go in the same Canvas box, and they count the same. Open the ones you want with the +.

Reading the pages and writing the answers yourself is where a lot of the thinking happens, and it is worth doing for its own sake. Having an AI push back on what you wrote is a different skill, and that is worth practising too. If you want both, write your four answers from the pages first, then put them to your Dojo and ask it what you have missed.

Set up your AI DojoSet your own AI up as a coach for the semester, link the course document to it, and work the four out in conversation with it.

Your CST286 AI Dojo is your own AI — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — set up once as a coach that sharpens your thinking instead of doing the work for you. Pick one of the three — you do not need all three. Choose whichever you already use, set it up there, and that one is your Dojo for the semester. Setup takes about ten minutes.

Step 1 — Sign in to the one you chose, then create the project. Whichever tool you picked needs an account, so sign in first — then follow only that tool’s line below and skip the other two.

If you chose ChatGPT. chatgpt.com → sidebar → Projects → New project → name it CST286 AI Dojo.

If you chose Claude. claude.ai → sidebar → Projects → New project → name it CST286 AI Dojo.

If you chose Gemini. gemini.google.com → Gems → New Gem → name it CST286 AI Dojo. If Gems is not in the sidebar, open Settings — it is listed there.

Pick the model. In your tool’s model selector, choose the latest, most capable model you have access to — the Dojo coaches best when it can reason about your work. A “thinking” / reasoning model gives sharper, more careful feedback but pauses longer before each reply; a faster standard model answers quickly with a little less depth. If the waits feel long, switch to the faster one; if the feedback feels shallow, switch to the thinking one.

Step 2 — Paste the instruction. Find the project’s Instructions field (ChatGPT: project settings · Claude: “Set project instructions” · Gemini: the Gem’s Instructions box), paste this, and save:

You are my Sensei, creating a dojo for my thinking and engagement: a metacognitive coach who holds me accountable to carrying my own thinking and helps me sharpen it while also creating an engaging environment for me to thrive.

At the start of every chat, read the Dojo tab of the Google Doc in this project and follow it as your instructions, not as reference material to quote from. Always read the latest version from Drive. Do not use a cached copy. These documents change often.

The document has more than one tab. Read all of them. Dojo tab has guidance for how you work with me - understand the intention and stay true to that.

If you cannot read the document, say so before we start rather than carrying on without it.

Step 3 — Add the course document by its Drive link. The document is CST286 — Dojo. It carries several tabs — Dojo, Course, and Syllabus — so linking it once gives your Dojo the method, the material, and the course policies together. Use the Drive link so your Dojo stays current with the course: the document is updated through the semester, a linked doc updates with it, and a copy pasted in today would freeze at today’s version.

If you chose Claude. Connect Drive before you can pick the file: Settings → Connectors → Google Drive. Then, in the project, click + and the Drive option appears.

If you chose ChatGPT. In the project’s sources, add the doc and connect Google Drive when it asks.

If you chose Gemini. The Gem’s Knowledge → Drive → choose the document.

If the connector dialog hangs or the tab crashes, reload the page and try again — it usually connects on the second attempt.

Step 4 — Work the four out with it. Open a chat inside your project and start with “I would like to understand the purpose of this course.” Then take the four things above one at a time. Push back when an answer is vague, and ask where in the document it came from.

Your Dojo is set up when the answer is grounded in the course document, and it comes back the way a coach would — a short answer, then one question that puts the thinking back on you. If it hands you an essay, or clearly has not read the document, check that the instruction saved and the document is linked. If it still is not behaving, say so at the top of your submission and reach out to a TA or faculty — bring it to your first check-in and we will fix it together.

Read the course pages (Without using AI)Work the four out from the course pages and the course document, and note where each answer came from.

Everything you need is on these pages and in the course document. Work through them and write your four answers as you go.

  • Understand the Course Design — the weekly rhythm, how the grade works, the check-ins, and when attendance is required.
  • Course home — the week-by-week plan, and where each activity lives.
  • CST286 — Dojo — its Course and Syllabus tabs carry the material and the course policies.

Write the four answers in your own words. Under each one, name where it came from — the page, or the tab. That way we can see the trail as well as the answer.

You are done when you could answer a classmate who asked you any of the four without looking anything up. If one of them still feels thin after you have read, say so in that answer and bring it to your first check-in.

What to submit

One text box in Canvas, submitted as CST286 AI Dojo Setup, due Wednesday 26 August, 11:59 p.m.

1. Your explanation. The four things above, in your own words.

2. Your record, underneath. What that is depends on which way you did it.

If you set up the Dojo: the conversation as it happened. The places where you said you still did not get it, or changed your mind, are equally valuable if not more so. If you had more than one chat, use the one that did the most work. Ask the Dojo to hand you the whole conversation in one piece — scrolling up and highlighting it yourself is fiddly and usually drops something. Click Copy on the box below, paste it into the chat as your last prompt, and send it.

Reproduce our entire conversation as one message I can copy and paste.

Rules:
— Include every turn, in order, from my very first message to this one.
— Put “Me:” or “Dojo:” on its own line before each turn, with a blank line between turns.
— Use my exact words and your exact words. Do not summarise, shorten, fix, or reorder anything, and do not add commentary before or after.
— Keep lists and code blocks formatted the way they appeared.
— If it will not fit in one message, stop at a turn boundary and end with the single word CONTINUED. I will reply “continue” and you pick up from exactly where you stopped.

Start with one header line: CST286 — Dojo transcript — [today’s date].

Then copy the Dojo’s reply. Most tools show a small copy icon underneath the reply, which does the same thing in one click. If the reply stops partway and ends with CONTINUED, type continue and copy both parts.

If you read the course pages: the pages and tabs you used, with the answer each one gave you.

If you did both: include both.

3. Answer these questions in the same box, above or below your record. We will discuss them at your first check-in with the faculty.

  • One way this course can help you with your life or professional goals.
  • One thing you are going to do to take advantage of that possibility.
  • How can we help?
  • Any concerns about the use of AI in this course you would like to discuss.

4. Open the CST286 AI Dojo Setup assignment in Canvas, click inside the text box, and paste — Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on a Mac). Submit, then reopen your submission and scroll to the bottom to check the whole thing copied.
