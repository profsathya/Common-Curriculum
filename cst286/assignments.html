Graded assignments30%
The work itself — nine across the semester, 100 points each.
Sprint Exams20%
Three at 200 points, hand-written and closed book in class (weeks 6, 10, 14).
Interview check-ins50%
Two to five sittings that sample submitted work; each sprint is worth the submitted points in that sprint.
