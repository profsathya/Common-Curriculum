# Write a physics question that tests your Sprint 1 learning

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Prepare for the Sprint 1 Exam by identifying what you should be able to explain about your physics topic and trying your question with a partner.

## Steps

1. **Write one exam question about your topic.** Choose a question that lets you demonstrate what you learned during Sprint 1.
2. **Outline what a good answer would include:**
   - The physical quantities involved.
   - The mathematical relationship between those quantities.
   - What happens to one quantity when another changes.
3. **Explain what your question tests.** What would someone need to understand about the physics to answer it?
4. **Try your question with your partner.** Ask them your question, then discuss your answer outline together. Ask your partner to pose a follow-up question about your reasoning, and write down that question.

## Submit

Include your exam question, answer outline, explanation of what the question tests, and your partner’s follow-up question.

You can submit your own summary, an AI Dojo chat log, or another format that includes these four parts. Paste your submission into the Canvas text box.
