What you get

Become a Self-directed learnerWhat it means. You can go after something hard that nobody assigned you — decide what is worth understanding, find your way into it, and tell whether you actually got there.Why it is the real subject here. Physics is what you practise it on. The learning-and-applying skill is what you take to everything else.Where it shows up. People like working with someone who can set a goal and reach it without being managed — in any context, not just this class. by exploring the physics behind something you are interested in. Learn it in a way that you can apply your learning in different situations, rinse and repeat to strengthen that learning and applying skill.
