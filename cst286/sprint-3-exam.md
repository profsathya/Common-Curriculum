# Sprint 3 Exam · What to prepare

Wednesday 2 December · In class · 200 points · Handwritten, closed book

This blueprint explains the reasoning to prepare. The exact exam questions and allocation of the 200 points are still being finalized for review.

## Purpose

**Builds on your earlier learning.** Your relationship, the check you ran, three applications and two look-alikes, and the feedback you discussed with your reviewer.

**This Learning Experience.** Show that you can explain and use a physical relationship, judge when it applies, and interpret the evidence from your investigation independently.

**Prepare you for.** Use your exam experience in My Sprint 3 experience to decide what you can now use and what you want to practice further.

## What you will be asked to do

1. **Explain and use your relationship.** State it in words and as a formula, define its quantities and units, and work a prediction from given quantities. Explain the assumptions that make it appropriate.
2. **Reason about an unfamiliar variation.** Consider a small change or a look-alike to a situation you investigated. Decide whether the relationship still applies and justify your judgment using the physical details. If information is insufficient, identify what is missing and what you can still conclude.
3. **Interpret your investigation.** Explain what you predicted, what you observed, and what the comparison supports. Distinguish checking a calculation or a model’s implementation from independent evidence about a real physical system. Identify an uncertainty or limit where the evidence warrants it; a supported result does not require an invented failure.

The type of reasoning is known ahead of time; the variation may be new. Practice explaining a choice of model, not only repeating a prepared calculation.

## How to prepare

Practice without your notes. Ask your agreed reviewer to discuss your relationship, assumptions and one scenario, then bring a new case for you to predict before the exam. Record how you used one piece of feedback: a revision, a reasoned decision to retain an answer, or an unresolved question and proposed check. If your meeting happens after submitting your scenarios, include that response in My Sprint 3 experience.

Use the same quality standards as the relationship and scenario assignments: words and formula agree; calculations and units are consistent; physical assumptions justify the model; and conclusions stay within the evidence. These standards apply to all three paths. Path A can draw on its spinning exploration, Path B on its independent investigation, and Path C on its reproducible build and tests.

## For your AI Dojo

Help the student prepare by inviting them to explain their own relationship and assumptions, then offer one unfamiliar variation without supplying the solution. Ask them to predict, justify applicability and interpret evidence. Follow their reasoning, teach at the point of need and let them try again. Do not write memorized exam answers or claim to know the final exam questions. The student completes the exam independently, handwritten and closed book.
