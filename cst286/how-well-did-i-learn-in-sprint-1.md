# How well did I learn in Sprint 1

Own your progress · Recommended Date: Monday 5 October

Take ownership of your progress — this activity is provided to help you, but the grade for this activity will not affect your final grade.

## Purpose

Reflect on how you approached your Sprint 1 physics learning goal to decide what to keep and what to adjust in Sprint 2.

## Choose how you will do this

☐ **I am OK with using the AI Dojo to complete this activity.** Tick this to do the reflection as a conversation with your Dojo; the page then shows the Dojo route below instead of the five boxes. Leave it unticked to write your answers in the boxes. (Page note: the tick is remembered on this browser.)

## Tasks

Start in Monday's class. Write your answer to each task in the box under it.

1. Describe what helped you make progress toward your physics learning goal and what, if anything, made progress difficult. Use specific moments from Sprint 1 to explain what happened and what you learned from it.

   **Your answer to task 1.** What affected your progress, with specific moments from the sprint.

2. Explain how important your physics learning goal was to you and how that affected the way you approached it. Describe one moment that illustrates the connection.

   **Your answer to task 2.** The importance of your goal and how it influenced your approach.

3. Describe what you and your partners did for each other and how that affected your learning. If you had little or no opportunity to work together, describe the circumstances.

   **Your answer to task 3.** Your experience working with partners, or the circumstances that limited it.

4. Describe how you used AI, if you used it. Include what you asked it to do, what it gave you, and how you checked its response. Explain what you learned about your understanding of the physics. If you did not use AI, say so.

   **Your answer to task 4.** Your AI use, your check, and what you learned, or a statement that you did not use AI.

5. Describe how you balanced your physics learning goal with other interests and responsibilities, and how your choices affected your progress. Based on your reflections, identify what you will keep and what, if anything, you will adjust in Sprint 2.

   **Your answer to task 5.** Your choices and their effects, and what you will keep or adjust.

## Dojo route (shown when the box at the top is ticked)

**1. Start the conversation.** Paste this into your AI Dojo, with your Sprint 1 learning goal filled in:

> I want to look back at how I went about my Sprint 1 learning goal. My goal was: ___. Ask me one question at a time and follow up until each answer includes something that actually happened, not a general statement. Cover these five things, in any order: one thing that worked and one that did not; how much I cared about the goal and how that care affected my learning; how my partners and I helped each other, or why that did not happen; whether I used AI, how well it worked and how I checked it; and whether I said no to something interesting to stay focused on the goal, or what pulled me away. Do not answer for me, and do not summarize until I ask you to.

**2. Keep going until it meets the bar.** The conversation is done when each of the five things has a specific moment from the sprint attached to it: what you did, when, and what happened. If you can only give a general answer to one of them, that is the one to keep talking about.

**3. Ask for the summary.** When the bar is met, paste this:

> Summarize our conversation under five headings: What worked and what did not · How much I cared and its effect · How my partners and I helped each other · How I used AI and how I checked it · What I said no to, or what pulled me away. Use only what I said. Under each heading keep the specific moment I gave; if I did not give one, write "not yet answered". Keep it under 250 words.

If any heading says "not yet answered", go back to step 2.

**4. Submit.** Copy the summary and paste it at the top of the Canvas box. Under it, paste the entire chat.
