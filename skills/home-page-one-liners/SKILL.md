# Home-page one-liners - the checks to run before `home.html` reaches Sathya

One screen. The rules, the before/after edits they came from and the version history are in `references/rules.md` in this folder - read that once at the start, then run this list on every entry's one-liner. Every line traces to a section there, named in parentheses. Read `writing-foundation` first. When a rule changes in `references/rules.md`, its test changes here in the same edit (and the other way round).

## On every entry's one-liner

- It names the understanding, capability, or experience the student walks away with, not the form boxes they fill in or a count of the items they submit. (Outcome over inventory)
- It states what the student accomplishes or demonstrates in this activity itself, never reducing the activity to exam prep or to what the next item in the sequence consumes. (This activity, not the next step)
- Every course concept is written in full, self-contained terms (`a physical system and its quantitative relationship`, `the physical conditions in which it is applicable`, `a physics learning goal`), with no form shorthand (`from what`, `the condition it needs`, `written as a prediction`) that requires opening the form to decode. (Full course terms)
- It names the concrete basis of the activity (`based on what you spend time on`, `how you verify your learning` rather than `show you reached it`), and where the entry has two halves — *how you learn* and *what you learned* in a sprint story, or *closed-book written exam* and *partner conversation* in an exam — both halves are named. (How it works, and both halves)
- It adds what the title leaves unsaid instead of repeating the title's words, and carries no filler tags (`actually`, `in your words`, `in your own words`, `your own read of`). (No title echo or filler)
